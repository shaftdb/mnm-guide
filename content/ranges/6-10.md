## Where to level
- [All-class camps 6–10](/leveling.html).
- Night Harbor outskirts, Underdocks, Tomb of the Last Wyrmsbane (3–15), early Shaded Dunes — maps on each [zone page](/zones/index.html).
- Detailed Shaman camps start at 11–15 on [Shaman leveling](/shaman/leveling.html).

## Spells to buy
- **Level 8 batch (must):** Pact of Renewal, Ghostly Blight, Pact of Haste, Spirit Mending. Nice: **Speed of the Wild**, Invisibility vs Undead, Spirit Bane III ([buy priority](/shaman/spells.html)).
- Keep earlier ranks; Pact of Renewal moved to L8 in 0.30.2.1.

## Gear & guild crafts
- Target easy BiS on [Shaman gear](/gear/shaman.html) (1–10 / approaching 11–15).
- Start feeding guild: Copper Bars + Rawhide Scraps toward Copper Chain / Copper Mace ([guild mats quantities](/shaman/guild-mats.html)).

## Gathering (Speed of the Wild)
- At **8+**, keep **Speed of the Wild** up outdoors (+40%→60% move, outdoors only).
- Copper veins + Wood piles: NH West/North Gate ↔ Shaded Dunes cliffs. Limestone from Mining 35+.
- Smelt ore → bars; molds are consumed.

## Quests
- [Shaman quests](/shaman/quests.html); [quests 10–30](/shaman/quests.html) as you near 10.

## Drops / mats to keep
- Copper Ore/Bars, Wood, Resin, LQ pelts, Rawhide Scraps, Jade/Emerald if mined.
- Chunk of Diseased Meat from NH diseased mobs — sale value UNCONFIRMED.

## Tips
- Solo pattern later: slow → DoTs → HoT → melee ([playbook](/shaman/playbook.html)).
- Riding available any class from **level 15** (wiki) — plan coin.
