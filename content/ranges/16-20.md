## Where to level
- **Ancient Crypt:** porter/cook 16, soldier/acolyte 17 — [Ancient Crypt](/zones/ancient-crypt.html).
- **Glass Flats** (20+): Muggrah camps 18–19 — very dangerous per wiki; group recommended — [Glass Flats](/zones/glass-flats.html).
- **Keeper's Bight:** Sangrel camps 18–22 — [Keeper's Bight](/zones/keepers-bight.html).
- Camps: [Shaman leveling 16–20](/shaman/leveling.html) · [all-class](/leveling.html).

## Spells to buy
- **At 16 (big upgrade):** Greater Omen of Lethargy (20%), Greater Pact of Renewal, Greater Pact of Haste, Sickness; nice Greater Ghostly Blight, Invisibility, Omen of Susceptibility, Berserker Rage, Ancestral Wisdom II. Tribal Rage needs Ritual Talisman (shaman scribe, Seekers of the Lost Eye).
- **At 20:** Greater Spirit Mending, Lesser Rite of Mending; nice Spirit Blight, Shrink, Lesser Regeneration.
- [Buy priority](/shaman/spells.html).

## Gear & guild crafts
- [Shaman gear 16–20](/gear/shaman.html).
- Move guild crafts toward **Bronze** when you have Tin: Bronze Chain / Bronze Mace (7 Bronze + Flux) / Bronze Kite Shield — [guild mats](/shaman/guild-mats.html). Still use Copper set until bronze is ready.
- Jade/Emerald jewelry upgrades with metal tier.

## Gathering (Speed of the Wild)
- Same SoW outdoor loops; push Mining toward Tin/Silver (Sungreet, Vale of Zintar, Scarwood — zone levels vary).
- Fine / Ironbark wood at Sungreet & Keeper's Bight.
- Iron veins appear in Fallen Pass / Glass Flats / Shallow Shoals (higher min skill) — gather when safe.
- [Gathering routes](/shaman/tradeskills.html).

## Quests
- [Shaman quests](/shaman/quests.html) (10–30 list). Riding from 15 if not yet.

## Drops / mats to keep
- Tin/Copper/Bronze bars, Fine Wood, Resin, Limestone, Hide scraps, gems, corroded/rusty gear for scrap.

## Tips
- Invisibility (16) helps skips and travel. STRATEGY
- Group value rises: you are slow + HoT + haste.
