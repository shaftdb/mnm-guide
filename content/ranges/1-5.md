## Gear & guild crafts
- Starter quest gear (wiki): Night Harbor Shaman starter pouch/tunic; see [Shaman gear](/gear/shaman.html) tab **1–10 (alts)** and [Shaman quests](/shaman/quests.html).
- Guild Copper Chain is overkill this early — stock **Copper Ore/Bars** as you mine for later. Exact crafts: [Guild mats](/shaman/guild-mats.html).

## Gathering (Speed of the Wild)
- Speed of the Wild is **L8** — not available yet. Walk nodes near the city.
- Mining: Copper / Rich Copper at Night Harbor gates, Shaded Dunes, Evershade, Keeper's Bight ([Gathering](/shaman/tradeskills.html)).
- Lumber: Wood / Rich Wood piles in the same outdoor zones.
- Smelt Copper Bars when you have ore + Crude Ingot Molds (forge).

## Drops / mats to keep

- Vendor junk; bank bars for guild.
