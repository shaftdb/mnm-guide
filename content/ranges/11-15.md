## Where to level
- **Night Harbor:** diseased bats (10), cracked/diseased beetles (12–14), decrepit→cackling skeletons (12–15). Map: [Night Harbor](/zones/night-harbor.html).
- **Infested Crypt** (8–16): [Infested Crypt](/zones/infested-crypt.html).
- **Tel Ekir** (10–35): [Tel Ekir](/zones/tel-ekir.html).
- **Underdocks** wererats (10–12): [Underdocks](/zones/underdocks.html).
- **Ancient Crypt** (12–22): skeletal guard/adept/inquisitor ~12–15 — [Ancient Crypt](/zones/ancient-crypt.html).
- **Tomb of the Last Wyrmsbane** (3–15): Wyrmsbane crusader 14 — [Wyrmsbane](/zones/tomb-of-the-last-wyrmsbane.html).
- Full camp tables: [Shaman leveling 11–15](/shaman/leveling.html) · [all-class 11–15](/leveling.html).

## Spells to buy
- Confirm **L12:** Consume, Omen of Enfeeblement, Root; nice Cure Disease, Dispel Magic; Consuming Pact later (healing reduced 2 min).
- Confirm **L8** still scribed (Pact of Renewal, Ghostly Blight, Pact of Haste, Spirit Mending, Speed of the Wild).
- Next big batch at **16** — save coin. Table: [Spells](/shaman/spells.html).

## Gear & guild crafts
- Band **11–15** on [Shaman easy BiS](/gear/shaman.html).
- Ask guild for **Copper Chain** (full set ≈ **108× Copper Bar** + **24× Rawhide Strap** / 48 scraps), **Copper Mace** (7 bars + Cracked Mace Mold), kite/tower shield, Jade copper jewelry — [exact quantities](/shaman/guild-mats.html).
- Named/all-class jewelry (e.g. Spitemaw's Ring, Shaded Dunes 8+).

## Gathering (Speed of the Wild)
- Outdoors: Speed of the Wild between nodes. Carry Mining Pick + Lumberjack Axe.
- **Mining:** Copper (NH gates, Shaded Dunes, Evershade, Keeper's Bight); Limestone ≥35 (Dunes cliffs toward Tel Ekir, Fallen Pass, Sungreet).
- **Lumber:** Wood/Rich Wood same zones; Fine Wood at Sungreet / Keeper's Bight when skill allows.
- **Smelt** Copper (and Tin when found) at NH Night Market forge. Details: [Gathering](/shaman/tradeskills.html).

## Drops / mats to keep
- Copper/Tin bars, Wood, Resin, Limestone, LQ pelts, Rawhide scraps, Jade/Emerald, corroded bronze, tattered rawhide.
- Do not vendor copper — vendors don't sell copper ore (BS wiki).

## Tips
- Solo: Omen of Lethargy → Ghostly Blight + Malady → Pact of Renewal → melee; Root if bad; Consume only safe ([playbook](/shaman/playbook.html)). STRATEGY
- Gather-only profession — guild crafts for free with your mats.
- Push toward **16** for Greater slow/HoT/haste and Invisibility.
