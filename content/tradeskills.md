:::note Patch 0.30.3.2 tradeskill changes (Oct 19, 2026)
All FACT from the [official patch note](https://account.monstersandmemories.com/community/patch_notes/62):
- **Mining:** Rich Tin nodes now skill Mining up to Iron (the wiki vein chart still lists Rich Tin trivial 135; flagged as a conflict). Rich nodes now give the appropriate amount of harvests. Harvesting checks inventory space first.
- **Woodworking:** the per-level skill cap is removed; it now caps at 300 at any level, like other crafting skills.
- **Leatherworking:** Straps are now crafted from Canvases; a Canvas to Scraps conversion recipe was added; tanning canvas yield drops from 6 to 4.
- **Failed combines:** most recipes now return either the merchant-bought components or a portion of the material inputs. Low-skill recipes got a minor reduction (the note does not say of what: UNVERIFIED).
- **Herbalism:** overly rare herbs got many new spawn points.
- **Poison Making:** Poisonous Spider Venom Sac can now be used.
- **Commerce:** sale-based skill-ups still only below skill 25, and now need a sale price of at least 10 copper per item and a buy-back price at least 25% higher (rounded up).
For this Codex's plan (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting; smithing off by default) the useful change is Rich Tin: it keeps giving Mining skill-ups toward Iron. Smelting itself is not mentioned in the note.
:::
