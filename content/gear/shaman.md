:::facts Shaman gear rules
Armor: Chain (and anything lighter)
Weapons: Blunt, piercing, shields
Stats: WIS > STA > DEX (wiki)
Bands: Use the Home / Shaman level-range selector
:::

:::strategy Ranking method
This is a heuristic, not a game formula. Each item scores AC×1 + WIS×3 + STA×2 + DEX×0.75 + STR×0.5 + AGI×0.5 + HP×0.25 + Mana×0.3 + mana regen×4 + HP regen×2. The weights follow the wiki's stat advice (WIS first, then STA, then a point of DEX). Each band shows the top two per slot among items the wiki lists as Shaman-usable, with an inferred level at or below the band's top. Effects, procs and resists aren't scored, so read the stats column.

**Lvl caveat:** for quest rewards, Lvl is the quest's *minimum level to start* on the wiki. Multi-tier quests like The Yowling (Ashira Pack Ring → Great Ashira Pack Ring) and the Cinder Beetle Armor Quests show 1, but the top rewards may need much more work and higher-level kills. Read the quest page. For named drops, soloability at your level is UNCONFIRMED.
:::
