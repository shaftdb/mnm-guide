## What to have crafted (Brewsham)

Ask guild crafters for these. Quantities are from wiki recipe tables. Molds/tools the crafter already owns are noted separately.

### Priority A — Copper Chain set (Blacksmithing) STRATEGY

Each **Copper Chain Links** = **6× Copper Bar** + Smithing Pliers. Each **Rawhide Strap** = **2× Rawhide Scraps** (Leatherworking).

| Piece | Recipe (wiki) | Copper Bars (via links) | Rawhide Straps | Rawhide Scraps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gorget | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Wristguard | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Cloak | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Gloves | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Mask | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Waistguard | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Boots | 1 Links + 2 Strap | 6 | 2 | 4 |
| Coif | 2 Links + 2 Strap | 12 | 2 | 4 |
| Shoulderguards | 2 Links + 2 Strap | 12 | 2 | 4 |
| Leggings | 3 Links + 3 Strap | 18 | 3 | 6 |
| Tunic | 4 Links + 3 Strap | 24 | 3 | 6 |
| **Full set (11 pieces)** | — | **108 Copper Bars** | **24 Straps** | **48 Scraps** |

Hand the smith **~110+ Copper Bars** and a leatherworker **48+ Rawhide Scraps** (or equivalent straps). Extra bars cover fails / components.

### Priority C — WIS jewelry (Jewelcrafting)

Wiki component recipes (JC page marked *out of date* in places — component lines below are from that page):

| Component | Mats |
|---|---|
| Wire | 1× Metal Bar |
| Setting | 2× Enchanted Metal Bar |
| Mounting | 1× Enchanted Metal Bar |
| Chain Links (anvil) | 6× Metal Bar + Smithing Pliers |
| Ring | 1 Wire + 1 Setting + 1 Gem + Ring Mold |
| Earring | 1 Wire + 1 Mounting + 1 Gem + Earring Mold |
| Necklace | 1 Wire + 1 Mounting + **2× Chain Links** + 1 Gem + Necklace Mold |

**Example — Inlaid Jade Copper Ring (WIS):** effectively **1× Copper Bar** (wire) + **2× Enchanted Copper Bar** (setting) + **1× Chipped Jade** + Crude Ring Mold. Enchanted bars need an Enchanter (spell Enchant Copper at L1) or Enchanting skill — **ask guild enchanter**.

**Example — Jade Copper Necklace:** 1 Wire + 1 Mounting + 2 Chain Links (12 bars) + Chipped Jade + mold → **13× Copper Bar** + **1× Enchanted Copper Bar** + gem.

Wiki "5-piece set ≈ 11 bars + 6 enchanted + 5 gems" rule of thumb **may be outdated** (page banner). Prefer summing the component recipes above; treat any shortcut total as UNVERIFIED.

Prioritize **Jade** (WIS), then **Emerald** (STA). Gem quality tiers (Chipped → Flawed → …) rise with metal tier on the wiki tables.

## Quick hand-in checklist STRATEGY

1. Copper Bars (big stacks) + Rawhide Scraps/Straps → Chain set + mace + shield  
2. Wood + Resin + LQ pelts → tanners → canvas/straps  
3. Jade / Emerald gems + bars → JC for WIS/STA jewelry (plus enchanter for enchanted bars)  
4. Tin Bars when available → Bronze upgrade path  
5. Corroded bronze / tattered rawhide → smith & leatherworker scrap loops  
6. Fine Wood / Limestone → higher tanning tiers later  

Gathering routes: [Gathering for Brewsham](/shaman/tradeskills.html).
