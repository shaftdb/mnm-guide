:::facts Gather-only plan
Role: Gather only (no crafting leveling)
Has now: Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting (ore → bars)
Travel: Speed of the Wild (L8, outdoors, +40%→60% move)
Goal: All gathering skills over time; feed a crafter friend or guild
Mats list: [Mats to give guild crafters →](/shaman/guild-mats.html)
:::

:::warn No crafting grind on a Shaman
The Shaman does **not** level Blacksmithing, Jewelcrafting, Woodworking, Carpentry, Fletching, Leatherworking, or Tailoring. Spells and leveling come first. Hand mats to a crafter friend or guild who crafts with your materials. Patch 0.30.2.4: tradeskills are expensive to start — skip that cost if a friend crafts for you. STRATEGY
:::

## What the Shaman does vs skips STRATEGY

| Do (gather / refine) | Skip on a Shaman |
|---|---|
| **Mining** — ores, stone, gems | Blacksmithing, Jewelcrafting |
| **Lumberjacking** — wood, resin | Woodworking, Carpentry, Fletching |
| **Smelting** — ore → bars (and scrap→bar if useful) | Any finished armor/weapon craft |
| Later: Herbalism, Fishing, Skinning (when ready) | Alchemy, Cooking as a leveling plan |
| Keep Speed of the Wild up outdoors | Indoor dungeon node grind without a plan |

Utility skills that are not a crafting career (Bind Wound, Survival bandages, Riding at 15) are fine when needed. They are not the profession plan.

## Travel buff for gathering loops

**Speed of the Wild** (Shaman L8): +40% movement at L8 up to +60% at L60, **outdoors only**, 60 minutes (wiki spell page; Shaman class table also lists ~35 min — treat duration as UNVERIFIED if they disagree). Same role as classic "Spirit of Wolf." Keep it up between nodes. **Speed of the Pack** (L32) is the group version.

## Smelting notes (you do this part)

| Result | Ingredients | Mold (consumed) | Trivial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper Bar | 1× Copper Ore | Crude Ingot Mold | 25 |
| Tin Bar | 1× Tin Ore | Crude Ingot Mold | 35 |
| Bronze Bar ×2 | 1× Copper Bar + 1× Tin Bar | Sturdy Ingot Mold | 50 |
| Silver Bar | 1× Silver Ore | Sturdy Ingot Mold | 60 |
| Iron Bar | 1× Iron Ore | Hardened Ingot Mold | ? |
| Gold Bar | 1× Gold Ore | Hardened Ingot Mold | 85 |
| Steel Bar | 1× Iron Bar + 1× Coal + 1× Flux | Tempered Ingot Mold | 100 |

- Station: **Forge** (Night Market forges; city forges). Trainer: Smelter Jadid (Night Market).
- **Molds are consumed** — one mold per bar combine. Buy stacks from the smelter vendor next to Jadid (not Underdocks mold vendors — those are jeweller molds).
- Scrap→bar and bar→scrap recipes exist (wiki); guild fletchers often want **metal scraps** for arrowheads — ask before you scrap bars.
- Vendors do **not** sell copper ore (Blacksmithing wiki). Your mining is the copper supply.

## Suggested SoW gathering loops (≈11–20) STRATEGY

1. **Buff outdoors** with Speed of the Wild, carry Mining Pick + Lumberjack Axe + empty bag space.
2. **Night Harbor gates ↔ Shaded Dunes cliffs:** Copper veins + Wood piles while moving between NH camps (bats/beetles/skeletons). Limestone on dunes cliffs toward Tel Ekir when Mining ≥35.
3. **Keeper's Bight / Evershade edges:** More copper + wood/resin; Whisperpine later. Align with leveling in those zones when camps fit.
4. **Sungreet Strand (when safe):** Tin/silver veins and Fine/Ironbark wood as Mining/Lumber climb — often higher skill nodes than NH gates.
5. **Bank bars and wood** in NH; smelt when bag is full of ore. Hand stacks to guild via the [mats page](/shaman/guild-mats.html).

## Later gathering (not yet required)

Herbalism (sickle, Sojourner's Camp), Fishing, Skinning when you want full gatherer coverage. No need to open crafting trees on this character.
