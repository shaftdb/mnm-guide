## Low-effort picks STRATEGY
| Skill | Why it suits a Shaman (or an alt army) | Where to start (wiki) |
|---|---|---|
| **Survival** | Bandages (Bind Wound), torches and campfires. Crafted from your abilities with no bench needed | Trainer: see the [Tradeskills](/tradeskills.html) table |
| **Fishing** | Pairs with Cooking, and you can do it while you wait for mana. Underdocks gained fishing volumes in 0.30.2.4 | Buy a pole from a fisherman |
| **Cooking** | Meals give temporary stat buffs and HP regen (wiki) | Chef Argna (Shaded Bloom Inn) or Cooking Trainer Violyn (Night Harbor) |
| **Bind Wound** | A free channeled HoT between pulls | Use bandages from Survival |
| **Herbalism → Alchemy** | Gather herbs while leveling (sickle in the primary slot), and an alchemist alt can turn them into buffs | Herbalism trainer in Sojourner's Camp. Alchemist Vizyl in Blackfeather Bazaar |
| **Riding** | Available to any class from level 15 | Rider Ebraim, Sojourner's Camp |

Skip for now: **Brewing** (the wiki marks it not implemented) and any skill with an "Example" recipe table on the wiki.

Full status of every skill: [Tradeskills](/tradeskills.html).
