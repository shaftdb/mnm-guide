=== 11–15
## 11–15: get your level-12 kit working
- **Spells:** Consume, Omen of Enfeeblement and Root (12). Keep Omen of Lethargy (10%) and Pact of Renewal (8) current.
- **Solo pattern (wiki Shaman Guides):** slow, Malady, keep a HoT up, melee. Use Root to make space if a fight goes wrong.
- **Night Harbor** (wiki levels): diseased bats 10, cracked beetle 12, diseased beetle 14, decrepit/risen/stumbling/cackling skeletons 12–15. The diseased mobs drop *Chunk of Diseased Meat*.
- **Infested Crypt** (wiki 8–16): crypt spiderlings, hatchlings and guardians 8–9; large crypt guardian, creeper and broodmatron 12–14.
- **Underdocks:** wererat fanatic, fiend, lancer and stalker 10–12; plaguespreader 8–12.
- **Ancient Crypt** (wiki 12–22, 8-minute respawn): skeletal guard ~12–15, adept ~13–14, inquisitor ~14–15.
- **Tomb of the Last Wyrmsbane** (3–15): skeletal fighters, warriors and cavaliers up to ~11, Wyrmsbane crusader 14.

=== 16–20
## 16–20: the level-16 power spike
- **Buy at 16:** Greater Omen of Lethargy (20%), Greater Pact of Renewal, Greater Pact of Haste, Sickness, Greater Ghostly Blight, Invisibility.
- **Ancient Crypt:** skeletal porter and ghastly cook 16, skeletal soldier and acolyte 17.
- **Glass Flats:** the Muggrah camps (scavenger, robber, scrapper, stabber, raider, shaman and more) are all 18–19 on the wiki. The wiki calls the zone "very dangerous", so bring a group or use extreme care.
- **Keeper's Bight:** Sangrel camps (arcanist, entangler, grappler, hunter, shadowblade, striker) at 18–22.
{{gen:camps 16,20}}

=== 21–25
## 21–25
- **Buy at 20 and 24:** Greater Spirit Mending, Lesser Rite of Mending, Pact of Restoration, Consume II, Greater Sickness, Ghostly Miasma.
- From here, group content gets much more efficient because you're the slow + HoT + haste package. STRATEGY
