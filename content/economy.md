## Spell scroll pricing
- **Sep 30 (0.30.2.1):** each 5-level range has a set total cost that's the same for every class. Earlier scrolls in a range cost less than later ones. Shared scrolls like Gate have one low price.
- **Oct 3 (0.30.2.3):** cheaper for classes that have very few scrolls.
- **Oct 7 (0.30.2.5):** a smoother ramp from 1–60 and a lower total. The devs are "looking into giving some coin back" for scrolls bought before the change.

## Income
See [Farming](/farming.html). Evershade Weald has the highest income per hour of the starting cities, according to the devs (Oct 4).
