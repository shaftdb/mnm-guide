Faction guide

What raises or lowers each faction, from the community wiki faction pages fact. The wiki lists sources, not amounts. How much each kill or quest moves a faction, and the standing a city needs before its guards attack or its merchants refuse you, is not on the wiki unverified. Use "Filter this page" for a zone, mob or quest.

Cities and their factions

fact Factions the wiki says you can raise or lower inside each city or hub. strategy Before you travel to a city, check these. A faction you have lowered is the likeliest reason its guards turn hostile.

  • Ail'Vorith (Deep Elf capital / starting city): raise Ail'vorith Exiles, Citizens of Ail'Vorith, Citizens of Ail'vorith, House Drezzorith, House Hal'kareth, House Ith'vayen, House Vael'xirith, House Varros, House Velgarrith, House Xal'tharn; lower Citizens of Gizmopolis, Clan Deepgulp
  • Faelindral (City in the trees (Wood Elf/Halfling start)): raise none listed
  • Night Harbor (Starting city (Human/Goblin/others), hub): raise Azure Veils, Ba'Haara, Bends Garrison, Chartered Academy of Arcane Inquiry, Citizens of Night Harbor, City Watch of Night Harbor, Clan Bloodynose, Cult of the Living City, Gilded Claw, Langsdorfian League, Merchants of Night Harbor, Red Mantles, Residents of Ain Golot, Seafarers Consortium, Slaves of the Pasha, Smallfolk Alliance, Unseen Court, Vermahn's Brood, Zintari Giants; lower Ba'Haara, Circle of Scarce Rains, Cult of the Wyrm, Residents of the Bends, Serpent Sashes, Stewards of the Reliquary, Ten Hooks, The Pale, Vermahn's Brood, Waterdogs
  • Night Harbor Sewers (Under-city tunnels): raise none listed
  • Underdocks (Starting city (Deep races, some neutral/evil combos)): raise The Trembling Hand; lower Citizens of Ail'Vorith, House Ith'vayen, The Dreaming Dawn

Ardent Keepers

The Ardent Keepers are the beastmasters and rangers of Faelindral, sworn wardens of Evershade Weald's ancient forests and surrounding lands. Their oath is to preserve the natural order between the beasts of the Weald and Faelindral, as well as protect the land from encroachment. They have organized with the Wyrdguard to combat the Fellstone orc incursion.

Raise in zonesEvershade Weald, Keeper's Bight
Raise with questsBeastmaster Starter Quest (Faelindral), Culling the Fellstone, Diseased Wolf Pelts for Baewyn Mossfang, Poacher's Knives, Ranger Starter Quest (Faelindral)

Ba'Haara

The Ba'Haara trace their roots back to Hathalia, fleeing its shores for Aêthoril during the Second Deicidium. Ba'Haara (ba-HAAR-ra), translated from Old Hathalian, means "The Seafarers". Ancestors of the Ba'Haara are believed to have been allied with djinn and other unseen forces.

Langsdorfian League

The Langsdorfian League's history goes back centuries, making them the oldest trade guild in the known realms of Eth-Ur. The Langsdorfian League is, in effect, a nation with its own military, spy networks, researchers and diplomats. The motto of the Langsdorfian League is "By Patience We Prosper". The Langsdorfian League and the Seafarers Consortium established Night Harbor as part of the twilight accord. Night Harbor is the seat of power for the Langsdorfian League, a confederacy of guilds with a near monopoly on trade and transportation throughout Aêthoril. The Langsdorfian League is named after the quaint village of Langsdorf, known for its skilled craftsmen and its role as the League's founding place. The Langsdorfian League maintains guild houses, known as "kontors", in or around nearly every city of note.
