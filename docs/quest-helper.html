Underdocks · Giver: Picci Manifold · Classes: Shaman

  1. The weapon progresses from Petrified Undercap Stem to Mycelial Staff, then Mycelial Spear. For the Mycelial Staff, complete Part 3, Step 1. The preserved-brain task in Step 2 upgrades it to the spear.

Part 1 - Starting Note and Shredded Cloak

  1. Give Moldy Note to Picci Manifold.
  2. Experience reward
  3. Faction: Rot of Gorm (+)
  4. Receive: You receive Shredded Cloak from Picci Manifold.

Part 2 - Four Blightspores and Petrified Undercap Stem

  1. Receive: You receive Putrid Fungal Balm from Picci Manifold.
  2. If you run out of balm, return the empty jar to Picci Manifold for replenishment.
  3. Use the Putrid Fungal Balm (4/4) on a sickly undercap. They will attack you. Loot Blightspores. Return 4 Blightspores to Picci Manifold
  4. Receive: You receive Petrified Undercap Stem from Picci Manifold.

Step 1 - Six Mycelium Roots and Mycelial Staff

  1. Receive: You receive Mycelium Sack from Picci Manifold.
  2. Gather six Undercap Mycelium Root and combine them in the Mycelium Sack. Known sources in Underdocks include level 5+ an undercap gatherer, an undercap scavenger, an undercap hunter, and an undercap truffian.
  3. Give the Full Mycelium Sack to Picci Manifold.
  4. Receive: You receive Mycelial Bonding Thread from Picci Manifold
  5. Right-click the thread, it will transform your Petrified Undercap Stem into a Mycelial Staff.

Step 2 - Preserved Specimen and Mycelial Spear

  1. Receive: You receive Specimen Jar from Picci Manifold.
  2. Take a hunting party out of the North Gate and travel East and find the tunnel that leads to a blight consumed mushroom patch.
  3. Collect the Blightspore Brain from a mindless blightspore, semi-rare spawn. This item is NO RENT.
  4. Purchase Embalming Fluid from Telar Nur'gta in the Underdocks.
  5. Combine the Blightspore Brain and the Embalming Fluid in the Specimen Jar to create the Preserved Specimen.
  6. Return the Preserved Specimen to Picci Manifold.
  7. Receive: You receive Jagged Chitin Spearhead from Picci Manifold.
  8. Right-click the Jagged Chitin Spearhead to attach the spearhead.
  9. Receive: You receive Mycelial Spear.
