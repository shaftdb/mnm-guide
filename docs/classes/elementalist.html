Elementalist

Elementalists are dedicated magic users who devote themselves to the study and mastery of elemental forces both on Eth-ur, and throughout the greater planes. An Elementalist is rarely without an elemental companion, and much of their magical ability is devoted to empowering or otherwise utilizing these companions in different ways. That being said, an Elementalist’s magic is very capable raining chaos and maelstrom upon their enemies directly. As a result of their interest and study of the elemental planes, Elementalists have learned the ability to conjure various useful, though sometimes strange, items with a myriad of different uses. Within adventuring parties it is most often the role of an Elementalist and their elemental companions to bring as much harm to the group’s foes as possible. Though, with the aid of their elemental allies, an Elementalist never truly adventures alone. ; ;

Wiki creation advice for Elementalist fact

Below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Intelligence is a Elementalist’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Your spell damage and mana total is directly tied to it. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point or two into Dexterity. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Dwarf: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Intelligence Gnome: +3 Stamina, +2 Dexterity, and +5 Intelligence Human: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Intelligence Ogre: +1 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +8 Intelligence

Quoted from the community wiki class page; it is advice, not a game rule.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Intelligent strategy

Your Intelligence is increased by 5%. Active: Become intelligent, increasing your Intelligence by 50% for 15 seconds.

Intelligent: +5% INT and a 15-second +50% INT burst.

Alternatives: Tough, Fire Resistant

Minor Combat: Conjurer strategy

Your Conjuration skill starts at 10. Your Conjuration skill increases 25% faster. ''Conjuration spells summon creatures and objects, and deal damage over time to enemies.''

Conjuration is the biggest tagged Elementalist school on the wiki (45 spells; another 45 have no school listed). Conjurer starts it at 10 and raises it 25% faster.

Alternatives: Channeler, Evoker

Major Non-Combat: Readiness strategy

You start with an available Cantrip slot, which can be assigned to a memorized spell to retain it after death.

Readiness gives a Cantrip slot from level 1, so one memorized spell survives death. Elementalists also have Summon Food and Summon Drink (wiki spell list), so Forage adds little.

Alternatives: Forage, Infravision, Ultravision

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

  • Functions as a pet‑based summoner with strong direct‑damage nukes
  • Commands an elemental servant that can fulfill unique roles based on its element
  • Uses spell‑and‑pet synergy to apply elemental debuffs, trigger elemental procs, and stack imbued effects

  • Performs well solo by relying on their pet to tank while casting from afar
  • Benefits from innate elemental resistances tied to their chosen school
  • Gains access to thematic elemental utility effects
What the devs say is changing

The Elementalist's core is staying intact. The update is about tightening the theme and adding depth where the class currently runs thin.

Direct damage is getting more variety, as each element currently only has one spell line. Elemental servants are also getting more to do, with deeper interactions between pet abilities and the Elementalist's own spells. On the conjuration side, generic utility items like food and bandages are being replaced with elemental-themed equivalents that better fit the theme.

More: Elemental servant interactions and spell-and-pet synergy; Elemental-themed conjurations; Variety in direct-damage spells across all four elements

Less: Generic convenience conjurations; Spells that don't fit the elemental theme

Solo: Official class page: performs well solo, pet tanks while you cast from afar. Wiki: four elemental pets by L12; pets need Crushed Malachite reagent.

Group: Ranged damage + pet.

strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Elementalist lacks (Magic DPS, Pet).
