Elementalist

Elementalists are dedicated magic users who devote themselves to the study and mastery of elemental forces both on Eth-ur, and throughout the greater planes. An Elementalist is rarely without an elemental companion, and much of their magical ability is devoted to empowering or otherwise utilizing these companions in different ways. That being said, an Elementalist’s magic is very capable raining chaos and maelstrom upon their enemies directly. As a result of their interest and study of the elemental planes, Elementalists have learned the ability to conjure various useful, though sometimes strange, items with a myriad of different uses. Within adventuring parties it is most often the role of an Elementalist and their elemental companions to bring as much harm to the group’s foes as possible. Though, with the aid of their elemental allies, an Elementalist never truly adventures alone. ; ;

Role & strengths

  • Role: Pet caster / DPS
  • Solo: Four elemental pets by L12 (wiki note since Beta 0.29); pets need Crushed Malachite reagent.
  • Group: Ranged damage + pet.
  • Stat points: Below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Intelligence is a Elementalist’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Your spell damage and mana total is directly tied to it. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point or two into Dexterity. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Dwarf: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Intelligence Gnome: +3 Stamina, +2 Dexterity, and +5 Intelligence Human: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Intelligence Ogre: +1 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +8 Intelligence

Design notes (official deep dive)

Elementalists command the forces of fire, water, earth, and air, channeling that power both into direct magic and into a living elemental servant that fights at their side. This update leans harder into that partnership: your companion isn't just backup anymore, it's a real part of how your own spells work.

Two new signature spells put that partnership front and center. One is a strike your companion delivers on your command. The other is your new primary nuke, that hits harder and gains a bonus effect based on the elemental companion you have summoned. Beyond those two, your companion's summon matches your level rather than falling behind, and the buffs and heals that keep your companion fighting have grown alongside everything else.

As MnM's most pet-focused class, you'll want to rely on the Companion Strike as your main damage dealer. It is the biggest hit for the mana cost, and upgrades more often than your classic-style Blast nuke. The Blast is still helpful, both for damage and the extra elemental utility effects it causes, but we wanted to increase the connection between you and your elemental pet as your main damage source.
