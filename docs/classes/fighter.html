Fighter

Fighters are those who strive to become masters of the multitude of arms and armor that one may find through their travels. They excel at being at the heart of combat, taking blows for their allies and striking back in kind. Fighters are found in nearly every culture one may encounter while travelling across the continent of Aêthoril. A unifying link between all Fighters is the courage to stand toe-to-toe with even the greatest monsters of Eth-ur. As such, their commanding presence in battle can control the flow of the chaos by directing attention onto themselves. Most often a Fighter’s equipment is chosen based on what situations they may find themselves in, meaning that veteran Fighters often aim to have a diverse arsenal at their disposal. Stat points

fact Class modifier: +4 STR, +3 STA, +1 AGI (wiki Fighter Creation Guide). Every listed race gets 10 bonus points at creation (wiki race pages).

fact Mana: No spell on the wiki Fighter list has a numeric mana cost.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Tough strategy

Your Stamina is increased by 5%. Active: Become tough, increasing your Stamina by 50% for 15 seconds.

Tough: +5% STA and a 15-second +50% STA emergency button.

Alternatives: Strong, Agile

Minor Combat: Shieldman strategy

Your Block skill starts at 5. Your Block skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Buckler.

Shieldman starts Block at 5 (+25% speed) with a Trainee's Buckler, which suits shield tanking from level 1.

Alternatives: Defender, Warrior

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Fighters may take it (Template:Traits). Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

For a Halfling: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

For a Wood Elf: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Medic strategy

Your Bind Wound skill starts at 25 and your Survival skill starts at 5. Your Bind Wound skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Survival Satchel and 20 Trainee's Cloth Bandages.

Medic starts Bind Wound at 25 with bandages: Fighters have no heals on the wiki spell list, so this cuts downtime most. Miner if you want coin first.

Alternatives: Miner, Lumberjack

      Tips

      fact Official class page — defining traits:

      • Fights exclusively with melee weapons, utilizing a shield for tanking and dual-wield or two‑handed weapons for damage
      • Has a strong toolkit of physical debuffs and preventative counters to incoming damage
      • Operates on a shared cooldown pool that forces meaningful choices in each combat cycle, with stances that shift the kit toward offense, defense, or control
      • Performs best against melee opponents and gains additional value when defending due to retaliatory mechanics
      • Possesses the strongest aggro‑control tools in the roster, enabling unmatched threat generation and stability
      What the devs say is changing

      The Fighter is being reshaped into a dedicated tank rather than one that also competes on damage. They’ll gain a broader aggro and survival toolkit, more ways to counter incoming damage, and a stronger retaliation suite. Overall damage output comes down in exchange.

      More: Taunts and threat-management tools; Tanking problem solvers; Retaliation abilities

      Less: Melee DPS, but still competitive

      Solo: Wiki: most comfortable in a group.

      Group: Premier tank: stances, Retaliation/Vengeance (deep dive). Kick & Shield Bash share a cooldown (0.30.2.1).

      strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Fighter lacks (Tank, Melee DPS).
