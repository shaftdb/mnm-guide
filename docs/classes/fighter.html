Fighter

Fighters are those who strive to become masters of the multitude of arms and armor that one may find through their travels. They excel at being at the heart of combat, taking blows for their allies and striking back in kind. Fighters are found in nearly every culture one may encounter while travelling across the continent of Aêthoril. A unifying link between all Fighters is the courage to stand toe-to-toe with even the greatest monsters of Eth-ur. As such, their commanding presence in battle can control the flow of the chaos by directing attention onto themselves. Most often a Fighter’s equipment is chosen based on what situations they may find themselves in, meaning that veteran Fighters often aim to have a diverse arsenal at their disposal. Design notes (official deep dive)

Fighters are among the classes with the fewest abilities, so much of this update is additive and focused on filling out their toolkit with satisfying upgrade paths. We’re introducing retaliation abilities and the Vengeance mechanic to their kit, allowing Fighters to continue dealing damage while solo or tanking for a group. Most of their current abilities remain intact, but expect some balancing adjustments as we tune numbers between classes. To set your expectations, we’re aiming for Fighters to do more DPS than most tanks, but less than a DPS-centric class like Ranger or Monk.

As a “master of arms”-themed class, Fighters now have a number of abilities requiring a specific weapon type. You’ll want to choose a loadout based on your weapons, or alternatively, bring multiple weapons and swap based on the encounter.

There’s also been a shake-up in regards to cooldowns. All Shouts are now on a shared cooldown, while Taunt no longer shares a cooldown with Unnerving Shout. Shield Bash is now on a cooldown with Shield Block instead of Frenzy.

Retaliation / Vengeance

This is a new mechanic specific to Fighters, granted automatically while in any of their three stances. When taking damage, you’ll have a chance to generate a stack of the Vengeance buff, which you can spend on a few abilities:

  • Frenzy - Eviscerate your target with a frenzy of attacks, dealing Weapon Ratio Damage up to five times and increasing your Attack Speed. (This ability consumes all your Vengeance, performing a weapon strike for each stack consumed. It has a higher baseline damage than before, so the hits should feel a bit more satisfying.)
  • Cleave - Cleave through targets in a frontal cone, dealing Weapon Ratio Damage plus Physical Damage. (This 2H-based ability consumes one stack of Vengeance to hit up to three targets.)
  • Second Wind - Catch a second wind, restoring Health every few seconds. (This ability consumes one stack of Vengeance in return for a short-term regen. Vengeance stacks fade relatively quickly, so using this after a fight is a good way to burn any leftover stacks. The amount healed scales with your Stamina stat.)

Stances

Fighters retain their Offensive and Defensive stances, while gaining a third:

  • Tactician Stance - Shift into a tactician's stance, increasing your resistance to Root, Stun, and Incapacitate effects. (This stance is useful for pulling, specific encounters, and likely PvP.)
