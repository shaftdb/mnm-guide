Druid

Druids are protectors of the natural balance of Eth-ur. They are primarily spellcasters. Druids are adept healers, skillful augmenters, and competent wielders of nature’s destructive powers. Druidic magic on Eth-ur primarily employs elements from nature, but may harness astral powers as well. Offensively, these powers reflect nature’s most destructive elements. Situationally, the Druid truly shines: they may also erode a foe’s resistances to spells, provide various magical enhancements to allies, summon the aid of natural beings, and even harness their connection to other planes to facilitate teleportation. A Druid’s diverse capabilities allow them to fulfill many different roles amongst adventuring parties. Wiki creation advice for Druid fact

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Wisdom is a Druid’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Wisdom is directly tied to your healing spells effectiveness and total mana. After that, prioritize Charisma. It helps with the Druid's charm line of spells, along with a few other spell lines. Finally, put a point into Dexterity if you have any left. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Goblin: +1 Dexterity, +6 Wisdom, and +3 Charisma Halfling: +3 Dexterity, +3 Wisdom, and +4 Charisma Human: +2 Dexterity, +7 Wisdom, and +1 Charisma Wood Elf: +1 Dexterity, +7 Wisdom, and +2 Charisma

Quoted from the community wiki class page; it is advice, not a game rule.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Wise strategy

Your base Wisdom is increased by 5%. Active: Become wise, increasing your Wisdom by 50% for 15 seconds.

Wise: +5% base WIS and a 15-second +50% WIS burst.

Alternatives: Charismatic, Tough

Minor Combat: Alterer strategy

Your Alteration skill starts at 10. Your Alteration skill increases 25% faster. ''Alteration spells heal and enhance allies, and hinder enemies.''

Alteration is by far the biggest Druid school on the wiki (82 of 146 spells): heals, buffs and snares. Evoker for the nukes (28).

Alternatives: Evoker, Channeler

Major Non-Combat: Readiness strategy

You start with an available Cantrip slot, which can be assigned to a memorized spell to retain it after death.

Readiness gives a Cantrip slot from level 1, so one memorized spell survives death. Forage is not available to Druids (Template:Traits).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner for early coin. Tracker (Druid, Bard, Ranger only) starts Tracking at 25.

Alternatives: Tracker, Herbalist

What the devs say is changing

The Druid's role isn't changing. They remain a primary healer with genuine damage capabilities and strong solo utility.

The update focuses on depth over breadth. Key spell lines are getting better upgrades and scaling, giving the class more to work with in the areas it already owns. In exchange, some of the more generic utility is going away, particularly where it overlaps with other priest classes.

More: Depth in core nature magic lines; Upgrades and scaling for existing spells; Unique utilities that feel distinctly Druid

Less: Elemental spells without a nature theme; Spells shared with the Ranger

Solo: Official class page: 'One of the strongest solo classes'; excels solo through control over animals and self-sufficiency.

Group: Secondary healer, damage, ports, Seasons system (deep dive).

strategy Duo partners: Shadow Knight, Fighter, Paladin, Necromancer — chosen to cover what Druid lacks (Healer, Magic DPS, Support).
