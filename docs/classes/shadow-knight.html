Shadow Knight

Shadow Knights are grim combatants who commit their lives wholly towards seeking out the greatest evils lurking throughout Eth-ur, no matter the cost. Their motivations differ: some search the darkness for personal power or fame, while others carry with them a vendetta or bright mission wrapped in a black cloak. Shadow Knights harness magic that originates from the very darkness they strive so relentlessly to combat. This magic primarily takes the form of draining and leeching spells, as well as spells which shape shadows to enhance the caster’s defenses. Within adventuring parties it is often a Shadow Knight’s duty to attract the ire of their foes away from their allies, though they are also quite capable at inflicting harm. What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Tough strategy

Your Stamina is increased by 5%. Active: Become tough, increasing your Stamina by 50% for 15 seconds.

Tough: +5% STA and a 15-second +50% STA emergency button.

Alternatives: Strong, Intelligent

Minor Combat: Alterer strategy

Your Alteration skill starts at 10. Your Alteration skill increases 25% faster. ''Alteration spells heal and enhance allies, and hinder enemies.''

Alteration is the biggest Shadow Knight school on the wiki (25 of 77). Shieldman if you tank with a shield.

Alternatives: Shieldman, Defender

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Shadow Knights may take it. Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Wears plate armor and prefers a two-handed weapon, but is capable of using a shield if needed
  • Has higher damage output than the Paladin, but less pure mitigation than the Fighter
  • Uses life and strength drains which can be distributed to themselves or the party
  • Casts fast, short-ranged abilities suitable for tanking under pressure
  • Can collect monster-hunting abilities from past encounters
  • Thematically distinct from the Necromancer: no pets, no death magic, and the undead are just another monster to them
What the devs say is changing

The Shadow Knight is stepping away from necromancy as its driving lore and toward the monster hunter antihero fantasy. The sinister tone stays; the undead trappings don't.

The biggest addition is the monster lore system. Collectable abilities learned from specific monsters give the Shadow Knight a customization layer that rewards experience and planning. Drains and transfers remain central, but the ability suite around them is getting more depth and scaling across the full level range.

The class is also gaining the same fast-casting, short-range abilities as other knight classes. It retains its role as the high-damage tank with the dark edge, but the dedicated pet and ability sharing with the Necromancer are being cut.

More: Monster hunter antihero identity and lore; Drains, transfers, and debilitating effects; Collectable abilities from monster encounters; Depth, upgrades, and scaling across the level range; Fast-casting, short-range abilities

Less: Necromantic lore and strictly undead theming; Dedicated pets; Generic magical utility

Solo: Wiki: some success solo through clever magic use. Official: dedicated pets are being cut, so do not plan around a pet.

Group: Tank with higher damage than a Paladin and less mitigation than a Fighter; life/strength drains shared to self or party; fast short-range abilities (official class page).

strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Shadow Knight lacks (Tank, Melee DPS).
