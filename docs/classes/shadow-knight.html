Shadow Knight

Shadow Knights are grim combatants who commit their lives wholly towards seeking out the greatest evils lurking throughout Eth-ur, no matter the cost. Their motivations differ: some search the darkness for personal power or fame, while others carry with them a vendetta or bright mission wrapped in a black cloak. Shadow Knights harness magic that originates from the very darkness they strive so relentlessly to combat. This magic primarily takes the form of draining and leeching spells, as well as spells which shape shadows to enhance the caster’s defenses. Within adventuring parties it is often a Shadow Knight’s duty to attract the ire of their foes away from their allies, though they are also quite capable at inflicting harm.     Steps fact
    1. Read the Imbued Parchment and hand it to Toilmaster Verith or Toilmistress Rallvessa.
    2. Use /look crate and /take pickaxe on the crate labelled REPAIRED to get a Slave Pickaxe (the BROKEN crate's pickaxe fails and Verith slaps you for 2 damage). Hand it in; it is given back.
    3. Go south into the water and follow the wall right to a small quarry cave. Equip the pickaxe and mine 20 Fragile Quarry Rock (nodes respawn in 5-10 seconds).
    4. Hand in the 20 rock and the pickaxe for a Pack of Stone (you become encumbered). Carry it across the footbridge to Supplier Jul, the first building on the left in the Foreign Quarter.
    5. Agree to make sharpening stones ('I will do it'). You get a Sharpening Stone Satchel and Recipe: Fragile Sharpening Stone (right-click to learn). Take 5-8 Fragile Quarry Rock from the crate next to Jul (/take rock).
    6. Through the market gate, right through the tunnel, right fork, first building on the right: use the grinding wheel by Blacksmith Zorvek to make 5 Fragile Sharpening Stones. Put them in the satchel and combine.
    7. Return the Full Sharpening Stone Satchel to Jul for the Sharpening Stone Delivery Satchel, then give it to Verith. Answer that you made them, then that you are prepared, to receive a Small Leather Hunting Satchel.
    8. Hunt the newbie yard for 2 each: Bone Chips (a decayed skeleton), Deep Beetle Legs (a small beetle, a deep beetle), Damaged Fungus Cap (a cave sporeling). Put them in the satchel in single stacks and combine.
    9. Hand the Filled Leather Hunting Satchel to Verith for the Satchel of Questionable Spoils. Deliver it to Cook Vashuk (inn past the courtyard fountain, back-right doorway, downstairs, kitchen).
    10. Hail Verith, say Yes to rejoining the Houses, and take Verith's Recommendation to Provost Xan'shorn (walks in circles on the left of the courtyard).
    11. He clasps the Obsidian Shackle on your wrist. Enter the Obsidian Academy and find your House representative (see the House quest for your class).

What the devs say is changing

The Shadow Knight is stepping away from necromancy as its driving lore and toward the monster hunter antihero fantasy. The sinister tone stays; the undead trappings don't.

The biggest addition is the monster lore system. Collectable abilities learned from specific monsters give the Shadow Knight a customization layer that rewards experience and planning. Drains and transfers remain central, but the ability suite around them is getting more depth and scaling across the full level range.

The class is also gaining the same fast-casting, short-range abilities as other knight classes. It retains its role as the high-damage tank with the dark edge, but the dedicated pet and ability sharing with the Necromancer are being cut.

More: Monster hunter antihero identity and lore; Drains, transfers, and debilitating effects; Collectable abilities from monster encounters; Depth, upgrades, and scaling across the level range; Fast-casting, short-range abilities

Less: Necromantic lore and strictly undead theming; Dedicated pets; Generic magical utility

Design notes (official deep dive)

The Shadow Knight embodies the anti-hero fantasy of a dark and brooding knight, bending souls to their will and learning more dark magic from the study of monstrous creatures. Shadow Knights use drains and debilitations of the body and soul to gain an edge over their adversaries, and in combat use their life draining abilities to supplement a combat style more focused on offense than defense.

In this update, we’ve tried to separate the Shadow Knight from the Necromancer class without sacrificing the most important parts of the class mechanics. There’s some subtle theme switching: command over souls and corruption in place of undead reanimation, as well as custom versions of spell lines that make a bit more sense on a class that spends the majority of their time taking hits in combat. The future Profane Study (monster hunting) system will unlock a unique and flavorful way for these dark knights to acquire new types of power.

Pets

The three spell lines of pets shared with the Necromancer have been replaced with a singular pet - a lost soul bound to the service of the Shadow Knight. This Soul Servant is now summoned using a Soul Remnant reagent you’ll siphon from enemies, rather than buying bone chips in a market.

The summoning spells themselves are set up in 4 ranks, creating pets that scale with your level (if a bit behind compared to more dedicated pet classes).

Melee Abilities

Shadow Strike replaces Slam as the basic Shadow Knight melee ability, making room for Siphoning Slash and Reave as the more powerful and longer cooldown abilities.

Shadow Strike - This attack extends the duration of any of your Shadows dot spells on the target by 6 seconds.

Offensive Spells

Soulrend - The lifetap abilities shared with Necromancer have been slightly tweaked into the Soulrend line. These lifetaps cast quickly, but have a shorter range than the Life Drain line had.

Palpitation - This new DOT replaces the Poison line shared with Necromancers and is a combination agility debuff and damage spell. The damage on this spell ramps up over 4 ticks, but deals its full damage faster than the Poison line. The lack of front-loaded damage and ramp up compensate for the fast cast time on the line.

Grip of Agony

Grip of Agony has had a substantial bump in its base damage, and now has an additional Stamina based scaling component. Additionally, when under an Unholy Pact from your Aura of the Unholy, Grip of Agony will heal you for 50% of the damage done.

Auras

Shadow Knights continue to have the two Auras: Vampirism and Unholy. These two had significant overlap in that they both increased the group’s damage with a health regaining component. Additionally, Unholy was a bit off-theme for Shadow Knight, which shouldn’t be buffing regeneration outright to their group.

Aura of Vampirism - This Aura is unchanged, and provides each member of the group a chance to cast a lifedrain attack on a melee hit

Buffs

Lord of Shadows - This self only buff replaces the Spellshield line, granting you increased Intelligence, Armor Class, Hit Points, and Mana Regeneration. Your physical stat buffs will come via your Theft of Vigor drain spell.

Wreath of Souls - A new ability and way to use your pet. Sacrifice your pet to place a whirling wreath of souls around you, damaging those who attack you.

Profane Study

A new theme and mechanic being layered onto the Shadow Knight is the hunting of corrupt entities to empower the knight. This is a planned future system that will be entering the game in a later patch and is not implemented as of the Early Access launch.

Shadow Knights will get a new skill, ‘Profane Study.’ It is used to study, understand, and control the unnatural forces granting power to the most heinous creatures of Eth-ur. By tracking down these abominations, slaying them, and studying their corpse with your Profane Study skill, you’ll be able to improve your existing abilities, or even uncover new forms of power.
