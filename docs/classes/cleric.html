Cleric

Clerics are those who devote their lives to mastering healing and protective powers stemming from their stalwart beliefs and discipline. This is most commonly, but not always, manifested through their faith in and service to a deity. Clerics are primarily magic users who specialize in the mending of wounds, curing ailments, and empowering their allies. Powerful Clerics may even bring their fallen allies back to life. Though Clerics excel at healing, they are competent melee combatants. Through strength of arm and unwavering faith, Clerics may smite their enemies with steel or employ powerful words and wards. Within an adventuring party a Cleric’s primary role is most often that of a healer, yet they can be fairly sturdy folk, and may take blows in place of their allies if the need arises. Bonus-point advice (wiki)

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Wisdom is a Cleric’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Wisdom is directly tied to your healing spells effectiveness and total mana. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point into Dexterity if you have any left. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Dwarf: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom Gnome: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom Halfling: +4 Stamina, +2 Dexterity, and +4 Wisdom Human: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom

Design notes (official deep dive)

Clerics are the premier healer: a versatile, faith-powered support class built to keep their group alive, while still bringing holy damage and utility to the table. With this update, we've given Clerics an entirely unique toolkit (several of their core spells used to be shared with other classes), rounded them out with a few new spells, and added some polish to existing spells.

Overall, Clerics remain close to their current form as a strong group healer with useful utility (cures, dispels, resurrection), a damage kit that leans into holy and anti-undead themes, and a few unique and valuable buffs. We've expanded several existing spell lines with more ranks to make the pace of upgrades more consistent, added new spell lines to fill in gaps, and removed a few spells that felt redundant or underused.

Clerics have also picked up a new starting trait: “Blessed.” As a class so tightly bound to their faith, Clerics enjoy a favored status, and thus may occasionally receive divine help on their adventures.

Defensive Upgrade

We've changed the way our armor class system works to provide more defense to heavier armor types. This means that plate armor is more effective at reducing incoming damage than it was before.

We've also given Clerics the Block skill as part of their Prayer of the Faithful self-buff. This increases the defensive benefits you get from shields by reducing incoming damage when you block a hit.

Damage

Clerics aren't a DPS class, but their holy magic gives them real teeth, especially against the undead.

  • Rebuke — Strike your target with divine energy, dealing Holy Damage. (This is your standard nuke that works on all target types. Formerly the Shock line.)
  • Smite the Dead — Smite a target undead with holy light, dealing Holy Damage. (This hits noticeably harder than Rebuke, both per-cast and over a sustained fight, but it only works against undead targets. It was renamed from Repel the Dead and its damage has been improved.)
  • Holy Strike — Strike down your target with holy energy, dealing Weapon Ratio Damage plus Holy Damage. (This is a melee-based attack learned at level 1, giving you something to do in combat besides casting. It also has a small chance to channel the wrath of your faith.)
