Cleric

Clerics are those who devote their lives to mastering healing and protective powers stemming from their stalwart beliefs and discipline. This is most commonly, but not always, manifested through their faith in and service to a deity. Clerics are primarily magic users who specialize in the mending of wounds, curing ailments, and empowering their allies. Powerful Clerics may even bring their fallen allies back to life. Though Clerics excel at healing, they are competent melee combatants. Through strength of arm and unwavering faith, Clerics may smite their enemies with steel or employ powerful words and wards. Within an adventuring party a Cleric’s primary role is most often that of a healer, yet they can be fairly sturdy folk, and may take blows in place of their allies if the need arises. Wiki creation advice for Cleric fact

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Wisdom is a Cleric’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Wisdom is directly tied to your healing spells effectiveness and total mana. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point into Dexterity if you have any left. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Dwarf: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom Gnome: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom Halfling: +4 Stamina, +2 Dexterity, and +4 Wisdom Human: +2 Stamina and +8 Wisdom

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Wise strategy

Your base Wisdom is increased by 5%. Active: Become wise, increasing your Wisdom by 50% for 15 seconds.

Wise: +5% base WIS and a 15-second +50% WIS burst, your heal and mana stat.

Alternatives: Tough, Holy Resistant

Minor Combat: Channeler strategy

Your Channeling skill starts at 10. Your Channeling skill increases 25% faster. ''Channeling allows you to continue casting spells through enemy attacks and other interruptions.''

Channeler keeps heals going while you are hit (starts at 10, +25% speed). The wiki Cleric spell list has no school column, so no school trait is suggested from data. Shieldman gives a buckler for off-tanking.

Alternatives: Alterer, Shieldman

Major Non-Combat: Readiness strategy

You start with an available Cantrip slot, which can be assigned to a memorized spell to retain it after death.

Readiness gives a Cantrip slot from level 1, so one memorized spell survives death. Good insurance for a healer.

Alternatives: Forage, Infravision, Ultravision

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

    Tips

    fact Official class page — defining traits:

    • Premier healing class
    • Uses prayer-based healing and divine magic
    • Wears heavy armor and a shield while bearing blunt melee weaponry
    • Capable of off-tanking; their heavy armor lets them absorb hits while casting
    • Has access to resurrection spells, powerful heals, and defensive buffs
    • Has dedicated anti-undead spells
    What the devs say is changing

    The biggest additions to the Cleric are on the healing side, including a larger toolkit with more situational options instead of more of the same. It’s also receiving a clearer separation from the Paladin; where Clerics channel divine power through word and prayer, Paladins do so through martial devotion. Spells that blurred that line are going away.

    Their heavy armor is also going to play a bigger role. They’ll be able to take hits while casting, and that's being leaned into rather than treated as a footnote.

    More: Situational options in their toolkit of heals; Durability from their armor, capable of absorbing hits while casting; Unique utility that separates them from other healers

    Less: Spells and identity shared with the Paladin

    Solo: Wiki: may find difficulty alone except vs. undead.

    Group: Premier healer: heals, cures, dispels, resurrection, Hymn/Blessing of Smiting group buff (L4, patch 0.30.2.1).

    strategy Duo partners: Shadow Knight, Fighter, Paladin, Necromancer — chosen to cover what Cleric lacks (Healer, Support).
