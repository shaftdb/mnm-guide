Stat points

fact Class modifier: +2 STR, +3 DEX, +3 INT (wiki Spellblade Creation Guide). Every listed race gets 10 bonus points at creation (wiki race pages).

fact Mana: Your mana stat is whichever of INT or WIS is higher (wiki Statistics); 56 of 61 wiki Spellblade spells cost mana. Deep Elf Spellblade: INT 22, WIS 17, so your mana stat is INT.Dwarf Spellblade: INT 19, WIS 16, so your mana stat is INT.Gnome Spellblade: INT 23, WIS 15, so your mana stat is INT.Human Spellblade: INT 19, WIS 16, so your mana stat is INT.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Dexterous strategy

Your Dexterity is increased by 5%. Active: Become dexterous, increasing your Dexterity by 50% for 15 seconds.

Dexterous: +5% DEX and a 15-second +50% DEX burst for hit rate and procs.

Alternatives: Intelligent, Strong

Minor Combat: Swordsman strategy

Your Slashing skill starts at 5. Your Slashing skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Scimitar instead of your default starter weapon.

Swordsman starts Slashing at 5 (+25%) with a Trainee's Scimitar. Evocation is the biggest Spellblade school on the wiki (20 of 61) if you prefer a school trait.

Alternatives: Evoker, Channeler

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Spellblades may take it. Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

Trait text and race/class limits are from Template:Traits; picks that your race cannot take are swapped automatically in the race note. Which trait is 'best' is STRATEGY; no sourced numbers compare them.

Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Light-armored melee DPS
  • Harnesses magical force across telekinetic, electric, and arcane schools
  • Shifts between different weapon infusions, each with its own damage type and proc effects
  • Manages infusion cooldowns as a primary combat cycle
  • Conduit discharges the active infusion for a powerful burst at the cost of a cooldown
  • Can place stationary arcane fields and explosive zones that damage enemies within range
  • Can teleport behind a target and strike, or blink away from melee range when pressed
  • Uses telekinetic effects including knockback, root, and enemy repositioning
What the devs say is changing

Updates to the Spellblade are about focus and depth: fewer schools of magic, more expression within the ones that remain. Elemental magic is going away in favor of arcane, telekinetic, and electric schools that fit the class more naturally, and the one-off pet is being removed.

The infusion and conduit system is the heart of the combat experience, and the ability suite around it is being built out to give the Spellblade a more interesting and varied damage rotation. Spell lines are getting better progression and scaling across the full level range, and teleportation is being treated as a genuine combat mechanic rather than a utility footnote.

More: Focus on the infusion and conduit system as the core combat cycle; Teleportation as a central combat mechanic; Arcane field and damage zone tools; Depth and scaling across the full level range

Less: Elemental magic; Off-tank focus; Generic caster utility

Group: Infusions, damage fields (deep dive); Kick back at L3 (0.30.2.1).

strategy Duo partners: Fighter, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Shaman — chosen to cover what Spellblade lacks (Melee DPS).
