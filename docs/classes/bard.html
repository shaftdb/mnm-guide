Bard

Bards are traveling musicians who learn to manifest song as a form of magic. Unlike the other great magic users of Eth-ur, who focus in a specific field, bardic magic is very diverse in its origins and capabilities. While a Bard’s song may not be as strong as certain magical spells, the experienced minstrel can bring to bear a solution for nearly any problem. Within an adventuring party a Bard is often sought out for their ability to enhance and inspire any allies. The time spent in taverns makes most Bards decent melee combatants, and quite capable of other roguish skills. When adventuring alone a clever Bard can find success in leveraging their wide range of skills and songs. Bards are capable of wielding nearly all weapons, instruments, and alcohol filled flagons, as well as wearing most types of armor.

Wiki creation advice for Bard fact

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Charisma is a Bard’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Because bards are often pulling for a group many players will opt to put their remaining points into stamina instead so that they can survive long enough to get back within range of the healer should problems arise. * Note: Mana total for a bard is now tied to charisma instead of int or wis like other classes. What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Charismatic strategy

Your Charisma is increased by 5%. Active: Become charismatic, increasing your Charisma by 50% for 15 seconds.

Charismatic: +5% CHA, your song and mana stat, plus a 15-second +50% CHA burst.

Alternatives: Tough, Dexterous

Minor Combat: Vocalist strategy

Your Singing skill starts at 50. Your Singing skill increases 50% faster.

Vocalist starts Singing at 50 and raises it 50% faster; the wiki spell list has 25 Singing songs, the biggest school for Bards. Virtuoso does the same for instruments and gives a Trainee's Recorder.

Alternatives: Virtuoso, Swordsman

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Bards may take it (Template:Traits). Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

For a Halfling: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

For a Wood Elf: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner for early gathering coin. Tracker (Bard, Druid, Ranger only) starts Tracking at 25 if you want to find camps faster.

Alternatives: Tracker, Medic

Tradeskills strategy

  • Mining: Gathering for coin; pairs with the Miner trait (this Codex's default set).
  • Smelting: Turns ore into sellable bars (separate skill on the wiki).
  • Lumberjacking: Second gathering skill for coin, part of the default set.
  • Bind Wound: Bandaging between pulls cuts downtime for classes without strong self-heals.

Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Support‑focused hybrid class with broad access to most spell types, including buffs, debuffs, crowd control, damage‑over‑time, and regeneration
  • Songs function as group auras that affect all allies within range
  • Songs have short durations and rely on twisting to maintain multiple effects simultaneously
  • Songs typically have no mana cost and can be used while moving
  • Has access to different families of instruments, including brass, string, wind, and percussion; each providing category‑specific bonuses (and some are required to play specific songs)
  • Can temporarily charm enemies as a means of crowd control, but not reliably enough to serve as a rotation option
What the devs say is changing

The Bard already has a clear and distinct role, so the update is more about filling gaps. Their current song selection is thin on the damage side, and instruments have functioned more as an optional bonus than a central part of play. Both are changing.

More: Depth and range in song selection, including a larger and more meaningful damage offering; Instruments as a normal part of play, including more songs that require a specific instrument type; Social and non-combat songs, giving the Bard room to contribute outside of fights

Less: Charm as a sustained playstyle

Solo: Wiki: 'a clever Bard can find success' solo using its wide kit.

Group: Songs (haste, resists, mana), mez/charm, melee.

strategy Duo partners: Shadow Knight, Fighter, Necromancer, Beastmaster — chosen to cover what Bard lacks (Support, Utility).
