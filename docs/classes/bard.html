Bard

Bards are traveling musicians who learn to manifest song as a form of magic. Unlike the other great magic users of Eth-ur, who focus in a specific field, bardic magic is very diverse in its origins and capabilities. While a Bard’s song may not be as strong as certain magical spells, the experienced minstrel can bring to bear a solution for nearly any problem. Within an adventuring party a Bard is often sought out for their ability to enhance and inspire any allies. The time spent in taverns makes most Bards decent melee combatants, and quite capable of other roguish skills. When adventuring alone a clever Bard can find success in leveraging their wide range of skills and songs. Bards are capable of wielding nearly all weapons, instruments, and alcohol filled flagons, as well as wearing most types of armor.

Bonus-point advice (wiki)

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Charisma is a Bard’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Most of your songs' effectiveness are tied to it. The remaining points are flexible, dexterity is supposed to be tied to missed notes but it is unknown how large of an effect this has. Because bards are often pulling for a group many players will opt to put their remaining points into stamina instead so that they can survive long enough to get back within range of the healer should problems arise. * Note: Mana total for a bard is now tied to charisma instead of int or wis like other classes. Design notes (official deep dive)

A common question after the initial Bard direction post seemed to be, “What exactly is the Bard’s role?” So, let’s begin by clearing that up. Bard is a versatile support class who focuses on supplying buffs that stack with other classes' signature buffs, crowd control, and pulling. The design of the bard forces them to choose just a few of the support facets they can provide at any one time, via their memorized songs, the songs they are actively maintaining, and the instrument they have equipped (and therefore the played songs that get amplified).

This update focuses on easing the amount of work required just to maintain buffs, increasing the variety of songs they have access to, and giving them a means to add damage to a group without maintaining a charmed pet. While instrument swapping is not directly addressed, the new casting mechanic and wielding style will hopefully reduce how often you feel obligated to do so, and this may see further iteration in the future.

Stat Changes

Charisma is now used to determine bard mana, as well as damage bonuses on songs. It would apply to healing as well, but Bards currently lack any such spell. Hymn of Restoration is a Health Regeneration stat buff, not an actual heal.

Skill and Wielding Changes

Bards no longer have access to the Dual Wield skill. While visually and thematically we tend to think of bards as capable of wielding a pair of fast weapons, the mechanics of the class are better suited to wielding a single weapon with an instrument in their off hand. Bards now gain the Double Attack skill at the same levels and caps they previously had Dual Wield.

Bards have lost the Channeling skill. This did nothing for them anyway, as their songs can only be interrupted via a stun.

Casting Mechanics

Bard songs which buff themselves or the group will now remain active after casting them and auto refresh after casting a new song. This has always worked this way for the most recent song, but bards are now able to maintain multiple. Initially, bards will play in “Cut Time” and maintain a 3 song Measure. At level 30, bards unlock the “Common Time” trait, adding a song to their Measure allowing them to maintain 4.

When you play another beneficial song and are already at capacity, the oldest song will stop being maintained and fade off quickly after. Alternatively, you can stop one of the active songs by using that ability, and start a new one without pushing the oldest out.

Instruments

Instruments have been greatly increased in strength, with basic store-bought versions now adding a 50% bonus to applicable songs. All existing instruments have had their bonus value reviewed and set on this new scale. Healing Hymn and Hymn of Inspiration have had their base values adjusted as a result of the new scale for instrument bonuses. The instrument types for some songs has changed in this rework.

We hope to see more spells impacted by instrument quality in future patches.

  • Instrument bonuses currently apply to the following spell types: — Stat bonuses other than Haste (including Movement Speed and Health Regeneration)
  • Damage Absorption Effects
  • Mana over Time (Hymn of Inspiration)
  • Damage and Damage Over Time (DOT)

Melee Ability

Bards gain a single melee ability, Whistling Blade. This attack does modest damage, but applies a Strength buff. At higher tiers, this buff adds Armor Class and Haste (Primary). While this has no casting time, it does apply a short global cooldown and isn’t best used when frantically twisting your songs.

Shouts

Shouts are mana-consuming instant cast abilities Bards can use while maintaining songs, or between songs they are twisting. You cannot use a Shout during the 3 second cast time of a song.

  • Bards retain all their current shouts: — The Screaming Vocalization DD line
  • Repel, an area effect de-taunt
  • Frightful Screech, the targeted fear
  • The Mesmerizing Whisper line of short duration mezzes
  • They gain one additional shout: — Paralyzing Shriek - Release a paralyzing shriek, stunning foes around you for 3 seconds

Songs

Songs are the bread and butter of the Bard toolkit, and are either twisted or multiple songs can be maintained in a set of active songs called a Measure. In this rework, some songs may have changed their Instrument type. Each Instrument type has at least one song that scales numerically with the quality of the instrument.

Charm

  • The Siren’s Call line of Bard Charm songs has received a few changes: — Only Beasts and Humanoids can now be charmed
  • Charms no longer have a chance to break when damaged
  • The Damage of Charmed NPCs is now reduced to be on-par with the damage of a dedicated pet class’ pet.

Mesmerization

Somniferous Serenade is now a Wind instrument song, but is otherwise unchanged. Mesmerization songs now handle overwrites based on duration, making it easier to work with an Enchanter to replace short lasting Bard mezzes with longer lasting Enchanter versions.

Damage over Time songs

The Dissonant Riffs line has been removed. This line included 5 songs that stacked, but it wasn’t obvious to all players that they could be used together and stack, and were often misinterpreted as upgrades that had the numbers or descriptions set wrong.

The new Vellicosa’s Fanfare line features two Brass-based magic DoTs that specifically call out in their description that they stack together. The higher level one, Vellicosa’s Tantara, adds bonus damage when the target is already affected by Vellicosa’s Fanfare. The total damage of these two songs is an upgrade over the total of the previous 5 DoTs - this is strictly an upgrade.

Haste Songs

These songs have been renamed to be more easily understandable by players that are not real life musicians, but are otherwise unchanged. In addition to their Primary Haste songs, Bards continue to offer top-tier Secondary Haste, allowing them to further buff groups that already contain an Enchanter or Shaman.

Resist Songs

Resistance buffs are an area the now Bard excels at. The previous two Magic Resistance songs that mimicked resistance buffs other classes get have been replaced with 4 scaling buffs that can be further improved based on your equipped Percussion instrument:

Bard resistance songs stack with resistance buffs provided by other classes.

  • Chant of Magic Protection - provides 5 to 10 Magic Resistance before the instrument bonus
  • Chant of Purity - provides 5 to 10 Poison and Disease Resistance before the instrument bonus
  • Chant of the Elements - provides 5 to 10 Fire, Cold, and Electric Resistance before the instrument bonus
  • Chant of Neutrality - provides 5 to 10 Holy and Corruption Resistance before the instrument bonus

Other songs

While versatility is a defining aspect of the Bard, the existing songbook was rather sparse, and this rework beefs that out with some new song types. Expect to see a few more show up in the future.

  • Beats of Battle - Beats of War has been moved to a higher level and the low level version replaced with this more modestly named version. Beats of War is an upgrade to Beats of Battle.
  • Aristam’s Steady Tread - Not every situation allows for the full speed of Bard’s Song of Travel, and Steady Tread fills the gap when a Bard finds themselves deep in a dungeon. The song offers a modest base run speed increase and the option to push that a bit further with an equipped drum.
  • Disenchanting Blare - A targeted Brass song to remove harmful effects from your party members.
  • Discordant Purge - The complementary song to Disenchanting Blare, this song dispels beneficial effects on your targeted foe.
  • Tembror’s Booming Bravado - Amplifies the voices of your groupmates, giving them a chance to fully resist Silence effects
  • Aristam’s Aerial Aria - This airy tune slows the descent of your group. While not making them immune to gravity or falling damage, it will decrease the rate at which they fall. Expect a pass on these levitate effects in the future to make them a bit safer, and use with caution for now.
  • Aristam’s Voluminous Voyage - This high level Percussion song combines the effects of Song of Travel, Sonnet of the Unseen, and Aerial Aria for the ultimate in bard travel.
  • Faedawyn's Piping of Protection - Weave a mail of musical protection around your group, increasing their Armor Class and absorbing incoming damage.
  • Anthem of Restoration - This high level upgrade song combines the effects of Healing Hymn and Hymn of Inspiration.

Retained Songs

Bards retain the following songs that haven’t been previously mentioned:

  • Healing Hymn
  • Traveler's March
  • Selstie's Aeolian Aria
  • Hymn of Inspiration
  • Sonnet of the Unseen
  • Harmony of Visions

Cooldowns

  • Bards gain one new long cooldown ability: — Crescendo - Raise your song in a mighty crescendo, increasing your Instrument bonus with all Instruments, as well as Singing.

Closing

That wraps it up for this Bard update. We’re looking forward to seeing all you future Bards when Early Access opens up!
