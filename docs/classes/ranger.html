Ranger

Archetype: Wilderness Veteran ; Roles: Melee + Ranged DPS, Utility ; Races: Deep Elf, Dwarf, Goblin, Halfling, Human, Ogre, Wood Elf ; The Ranger is a chain-wearing martial class whose identity is rooted in the wild. Their skills have been honed by a lifetime spent living off the land, not by channeling nature as a magical force like the Druid. What seems like magic to others is accumulated fieldcraft: old wisdom, remembered paths, and skills refined through years of necessity. In combat, they move fluidly between bow and blade depending on what the situation demands, and are capable of holding the line in a pinch. Outside of combat, however, is where the Ranger's full value emerges. They’re masters of travel, moving themselves and their group across terrain with ease. What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Dexterous strategy

Your Dexterity is increased by 5%. Active: Become dexterous, increasing your Dexterity by 50% for 15 seconds.

Dexterous: +5% DEX and a 15-second +50% DEX burst.

Alternatives: Strong, Agile

Minor Combat: Bowman strategy

Your Archery skill starts at 5. Your Archery skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Longbow, a Trainee's Hip Quiver, and 1000 Trainee's Arrows.

Bowman: Archery at 5 (+25%), a Trainee's Longbow, Hip Quiver and 1000 arrows. Swordsman if you open in melee.

Alternatives: Swordsman, Defender

Major Non-Combat: Infravision strategy

Your vision in darkness is enhanced.

Forage is not available to Rangers (they draw food from nature as a class ability, official). Infravision or Ultravision helps in caves at night; pick one your race may take.

For a Deep Elf: Ultravision (Infravision is not available to your race).

For a Human: Slow Metabolism (Infravision is not available to your race).

Alternatives: Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Tracker strategy

Your Tracking skill starts at 25. Your Tracking skill increases 25% faster.

Tracker (Ranger, Druid, Bard only) starts Tracking at 25 and raises it 25% faster; Rangers can track creatures (official). Miner for coin.

Alternatives: Miner, Medic

    Tips

    fact Official class page — defining traits:

    • Proficient with a bow and dual-wielded blades
    • Capable as an off-tank with limited aggro control
    • Can track nearby creatures
    • Master of snares and movement impairment effects
    • Has travel and escape abilities that benefit their party outdoors
    • Can draw food and unique supplies from the natural environment
    • Can enhance campfires to provide a suite of beneficial rest effects for the group
    • Offers out-of-combat healing and curing through natural remedies
    • Strongest in outdoor zones
    What the devs say is changing

    The Ranger is moving away from its druidic inspiration and toward a distinct identity as a wilderness veteran. The core remains familiar: sword, bow, and deep knowledge of the natural world, but the toolkit is shifting to match that theme. Generic magical utility and elemental abilities are going away, replaced with tools that feel physical rather than conjured.

    The fieldcraft side of the class is getting more room. Campfire enhancement and natural remedy healing are new additions that give the Ranger an out-of-combat utility role. Travel and escape abilities are expanding, reinforcing mastery of outdoor movement as a class identity rather than a footnote. Snares and movement impairment effects are becoming genuine areas of depth rather than incidental tools.

    More: Distinct identity from Druids and other hybrid classes; Depth in snares and movement impairment effects; Campfire enhancement and natural remedy healing as out-of-combat utilities

    Less: Shared Druid abilities; Generic magical utility and elemental-themed abilities

    Solo: Recuperate is for between fights; since 0.30.3.2 (Oct 19) it can't be cast in combat and drops when you enter combat.

    Group: Damage, Wound Care, campfire downtime reduction (deep dive).

    strategy Duo partners: Fighter, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Shaman — chosen to cover what Ranger lacks (Melee + Ranged DPS, Utility).
