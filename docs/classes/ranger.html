Ranger

Archetype: Wilderness Veteran ; Roles: Melee + Ranged DPS, Utility ; Races: Deep Elf, Dwarf, Goblin, Halfling, Human, Ogre, Wood Elf ; The Ranger is a chain-wearing martial class whose identity is rooted in the wild. Their skills have been honed by a lifetime spent living off the land, not by channeling nature as a magical force like the Druid. What seems like magic to others is accumulated fieldcraft: old wisdom, remembered paths, and skills refined through years of necessity. In combat, they move fluidly between bow and blade depending on what the situation demands, and are capable of holding the line in a pinch. Outside of combat, however, is where the Ranger's full value emerges. They’re masters of travel, moving themselves and their group across terrain with ease. The Ranger is moving away from its druidic inspiration and toward a distinct identity as a wilderness veteran. The core remains familiar: sword, bow, and deep knowledge of the natural world, but the toolkit is shifting to match that theme. Design notes (official deep dive)

The Ranger's fantasy is the Wilderness Veteran: blades up close, a bow at range, and a lifetime of fieldcraft that mixes a deep knowledge of the wild with some light woodland magic. Before this update, the class had drifted into something closer to a half-Druid, casting fire and borrowed nature spells between swings. This update pulls it back: the fire nukes, the fire and insect damage-over-time spells, and the elemental weapon and arrow enchantments are gone.

In their place is a fast martial kit built around your two weapons, four stances that change how that kit behaves, practical wound care, and a campfire ability that speeds your group's recovery between fights a few times an hour. What you were already good at stays: moving a group across open country, slipping past danger outdoors, calming beasts, and tracking your quarry.

You also live off the land, now foraging food, drink, and cureberries from the wild.

Swordplay and Marksmanship

Your combat abilities come in two sets, swordplay and marksmanship, and each set has an opener: Opening Strike for melee and Leading Shot for the bow. The two openers share a cooldown, so each time they're ready you pick one, melee or ranged.

Using an opener unlocks the follow-up abilities, then you can use one of them.

  • Swordplay Abilities — Finishing Blow - Strike with both blades at once. Needs a weapon in each hand.
  • Hamstring Slice - Cut at the target's legs and slow its movement.
  • Rending Cut - A cut that keeps bleeding after you hit. Landing it again before it wears off deepens the wound.
  • Marksmanship Abilities — Swift Shot - A hard-hitting, well-placed shot. Maiming Shot - A crippling shot that lowers the target's Dexterity, making its attacks less accurate. Barbed Shot - An arrow that leaves a bleeding wound. Repeat hits deepen it, the same way they do with Rending C
  • Tanglefoot — Snare several enemies in front of you at once. It only catches enemies, never your group.
  • Apex Predator — A short burst of accuracy and damage you save for the right moment.

Stances

The old Imbue Weapon and Imbue Arrows spells wrapped your attacks in fire, ice, nature, and lightning. They're gone with the rest of the elemental kit. Four new stances take their place: instead of enchanting your weapons, you change how you fight with them.

Three of the stances change what some of your swordplay and marksmanship abilities do.

  • Warden Stance - Melee DPS — Your melee attacks hit harder. Hamstring Slice also opens a gap in the target's armor for a few seconds, lowering its armor class. If your Barbed Shot is bleeding on the target, Rending Cut refreshes that bleed to its full duration.
  • Marksman Stance - Ranged DPS — Your bow attacks hit harder.
  • Maiming Shot also snares the target for a few seconds.
  • If your Rending Cut is bleeding on the target, Barbed Shot refreshes that bleed to its full duration.
  • Survivor Stance - Off-Tanking — Increases your parry and armor class. Hamstring Slice also slows the target's attacks.
  • Longstrider Stance — Shift to this stance and your group runs faster outdoors, as long as they stay near you. A member who takes damage slows back down for a while, so it's for covering ground, not for fighting.

Wound Care

You carry herbs, bandages, and poultices, and have the know-how to use them. It's based in woodsman's craft, but your healing spells require mana to apply.

Formerly the Weak Heal through Greater Heal line. The poultices now run from Weak Healing Poultice up to Superior Healing Poultice.

  • Regeneration and Pack Regeneration — Slowly restore health over time to one ally or to the whole group. Both lines arrive earlier now and each gains a new top rank: Exceptional Regeneration and Exceptional Pack Regeneration.
  • Mantle of the Hunter — Your personal protection: more armor class, a thorny damage shield that hurts whatever hits you, and now more Wisdom. It also lets you sense magical effects on what you consider. The whole line starts much earlier than it used to.
  • Barriers — Put a thorny damage shield on someone else. The Prickly through Thorny Barrier line arrives earlier now, and Jagged Barrier is a new top rank.

The Campfire

Between pulls the group gathers around the fire, gets its health and mana back, refreshes buffs, and enjoys a bit of downtime together.

Recuperate has a long cooldown, so you won't call a rest after every fight. When you do, it cuts the wait before the next pull. The cooldown also lines up well with buff durations, so the rest break is a natural time to re-apply them.

  • Recuperate — When the fight's done, gather your group around the fire. Everyone recovers health and mana quickly for a short while. You need the warmth of a campfire to call a rest - any campfire will do, including your own Hearth. You can't fight while you recuperate. You
  • Hearth of the Ranger — Build your own campfire. It burns longer than a normal one, and each log you feed it keeps it going longer too.

The Wilds

Open country is where you're most at home. This is the part of your kit for finding your quarry, feeding yourself, covering ground, and getting past what you'd rather not fight.

Your pack is never empty for long.

  • Tracking — Unchanged. Tracking is still a skill you train at your guild, and it grows as you level. The more skilled you are, the further away you can pick out a creature and follow it.

Group Support

Formerly Truestrike Aura. The armor it grants replaces the old Woodskin line.

  • Hunter's Focus — Sharpen your group's weapon skills and Dexterity, and harden their armor. It's now a group buff you cast instead of an always-on aura.
  • Protection and Resistance — Poison Protection and Poison Resistance, Corruption Protection and Corruption Resistance, and See Invisibility are all still here.
