Monk

Monks are skilled martial artists who devote themselves to shaping their bodies into deadly, yet graceful, instruments of violence. For many Monks this devotion is of a spiritual nature, though this is not necessarily true for all of them. Most Monks view battle as a grim necessity to further their art. Others view battle as a righteous and glorious end, rather than a means. Monks choose to face their opponents up close and head on. Above all else, Monks choose to master the use of their body as a weapon. With deadly blows, well placed kicks, artful dodging, and even the ability to fake their own death, Monks make for potent melee combatants. Within an adventuring group, Monk’s use their speed, dexterity, and bravery to draw in foes for their allies to fight. Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Utilizes bare fists, kicks, fist weapons, and staves in combat
  • Can feign death as a cornerstone pulling and escape tool
  • Can serve as an avoidance-based off-tank via dodges and blocks; limited in taunt toolkit
  • Has stances that enhance either damage, avoidance, or speed, with shared cooldowns that create meaningful combat choices
  • Has disciplines that provide powerful, long-cooldown bursts of offense or near-invulnerability
  • Has physical debuffs to impair melee and caster opponents
  • Capable of soloing due to their self-reliance and can mend their own wounds
What the devs say is changing

The biggest change involves the Monk’s combat choices. Their current toolkit is lean in a way that flattens moment-to-moment gameplay, so through expansion of the shared cooldown system, players will be able to make genuine decisions during a fight rather than settling into a fixed rotation. Ability scaling and upgrades are also being fleshed out across the full level range.

The other change is thematic. The elemental theme in their current abilities is being replaced with lore and aesthetics that belong specifically to the Monk. The class earns its identity through what it does, not through buffs it hands to others, and the ability design is being brought in line with that.

Design notes (official deep dive)

As mentioned in the initial class update post, one of the biggest change to Monks is thematic. They're pivoting away from the elemental theme toward a more traditional spiritual one. Most of the original Monk kit remains intact with the exception of Way of the Water, which has been removed with its associated group heal.

Styles

  • The four “Way of”-stances have been remapped to three styles: — Sun Style - Assume a sun-based fighting style, increasing your Offense. Additionally, Celestial Strike reverberates through to your target's soul, dealing Magic Damage after a few seconds. Moon Style - Assume a moon-based fighting style, increasing your Dodge

Feign Death and Pulling

In this patch, we fixed a long-standing bug that prevented any chance of re-aggroing mobs after standing up from a feign. Though re-aggro after a feign is now possible, the chances of it occurring have been massively reduced to allow Monks time to adapt. Considering how long this bug has been present, it'll probably take a bit of tuning to dial this chance in.

We’ve also introduced a new change to how NPCs loiter after a successful feign. NPCs that have been damaged will loiter much longer than untouched ones, giving you a little more control over the timing when they reset. Maximum loiter times have been reduced overall as well.

  • Pinning Throw - Throw a weapon at your target, dealing Weapon Ratio Damage and immobilizing them. (This is an upgraded version of Throw that briefly pins the target in place, allowing you to stagger one of the pack a bit behind the rest.)
  • Double-Handed Strike - Strike your target with both hands, knocking them back and decreasing their Movement Speed.

Mobility

  • Monks gain a couple mobility tools beyond their Wind Style buff: — Flying Kick - Leap toward your target to deliver a devastating flying kick, dealing Physical Damage. This ability scales with your Dexterity. (This is your gap closer.) Fleet of Foot - Hasten your steps, increasing your Movement Speed. (This is a short-duratio
  • Melee Attacks — Round Kick - Deliver a crushing round kick to your target, dealing Physical Damage and granting you a chance to reset the cooldown of your Celestial Strike and Hundred Fist abilities. This ability scales with your Dexterity. Celestial Strike - Squarely strike

Body Mastery

Monk training hones both the body and spirit, giving them a number of disciplines they can choose from. However, you can only have one body alteration ability active at a time:

  • Spirit Sustenance - Suppress the need for sustenance, decreasing your food and drink consumption.
  • Slow Breathing - Slow your breathing, decreasing your air consumption while underwater.
  • Falling Petal - Move with the grace of a falling petal, reducing the effect of gravity on your body. (This ability compounds with your Safe Fall skill.)
  • Ascendant Body - Ascend your body to a state of perfection, simultaneously decreasing your food, drink, and air consumption, and reducing the effect of gravity on your body. (At the highest levels of mastery, Monks can command all of the body disciplines at once.)

Defense and Cooldowns

Monks keep their existing Mend and Stone Stance cooldowns, while gaining:

  • Perfect Focus - Perfect your strikes through deep focus, causing melee attacks to temporarily land as Critical Hits.
  • Cyclone Staff - Spin your staff rapidly to deflect incoming blows, greatly increasing your Block chance and dealing Weapon Ratio Damage with each successful block. (This is a channeled ability that requires a two-handed staff.)
  • Iron Fists - Forge your fists into iron, temporarily nullifying the effects of Damage Shields.
  • Ethereal Body - Become nigh ethereal, increasing your Agility and Dodge chance.
