Beastmaster

Archetype: Animal-Taming Primal Brawler ; Roles: Melee DPS, Pet ; Races: Deep Gnome, Goblin, Halfling, Human, Ogre, Troll, Wood Elf ; The Beastmaster is a leather-armored melee brawler who fights without magic, instead choosing to have a bonded beast at their side. Where other classes channel supernatural forces, the Beastmaster draws on something rawer: physical power and a deep connection to the animal world. One animal fights alongside them as a persistent combat partner, while others aid them through their toolkit of bestial abilities. The Beastmaster is capable of strengthening both themselves and their companion, emphasizing the bond they share and their reliance on one another. What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Strong strategy

Your Strength is increased by 5%. Active: Become strong, increasing your Strength by 50% for 15 seconds.

Strong: +5% STR and a 15-second +50% STR burst for your melee.

Alternatives: Agile, Wise

Minor Combat: Alterer strategy

Your Alteration skill starts at 10. Your Alteration skill increases 25% faster. ''Alteration spells heal and enhance allies, and hinder enemies.''

Alteration is the biggest Beastmaster school on the wiki spell list (35 of 74). Alterer starts it at 10 and raises it 25% faster. Warrior (Offense) if you play mostly melee.

Alternatives: Warrior, Defender

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Beastmasters may take it. Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

For a Halfling: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

For a Wood Elf: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

      Tips

      fact Official class page — defining traits:

      • Wields two-handed weapons or double-fisted claws
      • Maintains one persistent animal companion that fights alongside them in melee combat
      • Companion must be healed, cared for, and revived if it dies in combat
      • Uses a taming mechanic to acquire companions that is distinct from summoning
      • Temporarily calls upon hawks, rats, and other creatures in specific abilities
      • Has abilities that can buff them, their companion, and party members
      • Has bestial-themed stances for different combat (damage, defense) and utility modes (speed, stealth, resistance)
      • Has survivalist abilities including self and companion heals
      What the devs say is changing

      The Beastmaster is leaving behind its roots as a Shaman hybrid. The spirit world connection, shamanistic abilities, and swarm pets are going away entirely. What replaces them is a more grounded identity as a primal melee fighter, whose power comes from physical mastery and their bond with animals, not magical tradition.

      More: Two-handed weapons and double-fisted claws as weapon options; Animal-themed abilities and contextual creatures throughout the toolkit; Primal stances that buff the Beastmaster, their companion, and the party; Beastmaster and companion-specific survival abilities

      Less: Shared abilities with Shamans; Traditional hybrid role, instead becoming a unique class; Swarm pet mechanics

      Solo: Pet tanks with Guardian Spirit for solo/small group (deep dive).

      Group: Melee + pet damage; pet roles DPS / Tank / Balanced.

      strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Beastmaster lacks (Melee DPS, Pet).
