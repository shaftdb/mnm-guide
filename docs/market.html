Market & prices

How trading works in M&M
  • fact There is no in-game auction house or bazaar. Players trade in zone-wide /auction chat, by /tell, and on fan sites. The devs have discussed consignment selling, with no date (forum, Aug 20).
  • fact Merchants mostly buy only what they sell, and pay a premium for it. Shady merchants buy anything for less. What players sell joins the merchant's stock.
  • fact Patch 0.30.2.9 (Oct 15) rebalanced coin drops across many NPC types, and humanoids now also drop sellable medallions. Values seen before then may be out of date.
  • unverified Player prices below are single asking prices or anonymous sale reports from The Tunnel, not market averages. Use them as a rough guide only.

Adding a price

Prices are kept in content/prices.json. Add new observations with tools/prices_update.py --add new.json --build: it checks every field, turns prices into copper, removes duplicates, stamps dates and rebuilds. Only add a price you saw in a source, with its link and date.
