Latest news

Notable Monsters & Memories news from official sources. Each summary is taken only from the linked source. Newest first.

  • Oct 15, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.9 (Module 1 Patch 12)
    Rebalanced coin/cash drop across a number of NPC types and fixed certain herbs missing spawning restrictions. Includes a Ranger Recuperate fix for a bug from the patch 0.30.3.2 reversion. — Official patch notes patch economy
  • Oct 11, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.8: Night Harbor reunited
    More fixes to economic exploits related to mispriced tradeskill items, and missing house crates brought back. Night Harbor has been reunited into one big zone. — Official patch notes patch economy housing
  • Oct 7, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.5: spell scroll pricing
    Spell scroll costs updated for a smoother ramp from levels 1-60 and a lower total cost to buy all. Purchasing Night Harbor East houses from the West realtor should now work. — Official patch notes patch economy spells
  • Oct 4, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.4: economy and stability fixes
    Fixed selling unsellable items for 0 copper, a possible client memory leak and zoning stuck in loading screens. Deep Beetle Eye prices raised to match Fire Beetle Eyes. — Official patch notes patch economy
  • Sep 30, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.1: Release Patch
    Module 1 Patch 4, the release patch: new login backgrounds, character creation camera adjustments, and NPC corpses above level 60 now last 28 minutes, up from 8. — Official patch notes patch launch
  • Sep 29, 2026 Necromancer, Ranger and Shadow Knight Deep Dives + Traits preview
    An overview of changes to the Necromancer, Ranger and Shadow Knight classes and a preview of Traits ahead of Early Access on Oct. 1. The initial class changes are in the October 1st launch build. — Official updates dev-post classes launch
  • Sep 25, 2026 Patch 0.30.2.0: Blind Midden opens
    Module 1 Patch 3 adds the initial Blind Midden zone and spawning, fixes an exploitable Rothold pathing bug and camping on lifts and boats, and adds Ail'Vorith content. — Official patch notes patch zones
  • Sep 19, 2026 Bard and Enchanter Deep Dives
    Beta Patch 0.30.0.0 includes the Bard and Enchanter Deep Dives, with much going on in preparation for Early Access. — Official updates dev-post classes
  • Sep 19, 2026 Patch 0.30.0.0: Early Access Patch + Module 1
    Most group buffs can now be bought from spell vendors instead of research, stealth detection is adjusted, and mesmerize overwriting now depends on spell duration. — Official patch notes patch launch classes
  • Sep 15, 2026 Get Ready for Early Access
    Early Access begins on October 1st. The team of 50 volunteers is prepping to enter this next phase. — Official updates dev-post launch
  • Sep 10, 2026 Elementalist & Paladin Deep Dives; the Deep goes live
    Beta Patch 0.29.0.0 includes the Elementalist and Paladin Deep Dives, and the Module 1 Deep zones went live, still very much under construction. — Official updates dev-post classes zones
  • Sep 10, 2026 Patch 0.29.0.0: The Deep unlocked
    The Deep (Module 1) is unlocked, with zones still in progress. Adds a forced solo challenge mode, and rotten corpse summoning moves to the opportunistic adventurer in all inns. — Official patch notes patch zones
