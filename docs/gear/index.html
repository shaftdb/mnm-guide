How "level" is inferred

M&M items mostly have no level requirement. The Lvl column is the earliest level you could reasonably get the item: the quest's minimum level, or the lowest wiki level of a mob that drops it. "any / ?" means it's vendor or crafted with no level, or the source has no level listed.

Removed items and Early Access churn

Items in the wiki's "Removed Items" category are excluded. Drops and quest rewards change often during EA (for example 0.30.2.3 removed some unintended low-level drops). Check the wiki link in each tooltip.
