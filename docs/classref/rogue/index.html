Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Melee dagger specialist
  • Master of thrown weapons, capable of landing critical hits with them
  • Primarily deals damage with backstabs, requiring careful positioning
  • Utilizes alchemical poisons, from which they can choose a variety of different effects, including debuffs, damage over time, and physical impairments
  • Can incapacitate an unsuspecting target without breaking stealth
  • Has the most powerful form of invisibility in the roster, capable of traversing areas impassable to other classes
  • Can break engagement by jumping into stealth mid-fight
  • Has more escape and evasion tools than any other class
What the devs say is changing

Poisons are the biggest focus. The update expands the poison suite into a genuine preparation system with more options, more meaningful choices between them, and better scaling across the level range.

The combat ability selection is also getting fleshed out. The current rotation offers limited room for skill expression or meaningful choices. That's being addressed with a broader damage ability kit that gives experienced players more to work with without complicating the core.

More: Options for poisons and meaningful pre-combat choices; Depth and variety in combat abilities; Ability scaling and upgrades across the full level range

Solo: Deep dive has a 'Soloability' section; poisons via Poison Making (Rogue-only).

Group: Backstab damage, pick pocket, lockpicking, disarm traps.

strategy Duo partners: Fighter, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Shaman — chosen to cover what Rogue lacks (Melee DPS).

Stat points

fact Class modifier: +4 DEX, +4 AGI (wiki Rogue Creation Guide). Every listed race gets 10 bonus points at creation (wiki race pages).

fact Mana: No spell on the wiki Rogue list has a numeric mana cost.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Dexterous strategy

Your Dexterity is increased by 5%. Active: Become dexterous, increasing your Dexterity by 50% for 15 seconds.

Dexterous: +5% DEX and a 15-second +50% DEX burst.

Alternatives: Strong, Agile

Minor Combat: Cutthroat strategy

Your Piercing skill starts at 5. Your Piercing skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Dirk instead of your default starter weapon.

Cutthroat starts Piercing at 5 (+25%) with a Trainee's Dirk: daggers are your main weapon. Knife Thrower gives 1000 throwing daggers.

Alternatives: Knife Thrower, Warrior

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Rogues may take it. Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

For a Halfling: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

For a Wood Elf: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Poison Maker strategy

Your Poison Making skill starts at 25. Your Poison Making skills increase 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Poison Making Kit.

Poison Maker (Rogue only) starts Poison Making at 25 (+25% speed) with a kit; the official page makes poisons a core Rogue tool.

Alternatives: Miner, Medic
