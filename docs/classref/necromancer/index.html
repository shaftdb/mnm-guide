Bonus-point advice (wiki)

Below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Intelligence is a Necromancer’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Your spell damage and mana total is directly tied to it. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point or two into Dexterity. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Gnome: +4 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +5 Intelligence Goblin: +3 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +6 Intelligence Human: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Intelligence

Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Pet-focused magic DPS
  • Uses corruption and decay as primary damage sources, along with life-drain effects for sustain
  • Undead minion functions as a capable tank, holding aggro and absorbing punishment while they cast from afar
  • Pet progresses from humble skeletal forms to increasingly dire undead types; unique pet types are collectable through world exploration
  • Can temporarily raise fallen enemies as additional pets
  • Can control, disempower, and bend undead enemies to their will
  • Drains life force, strength, and vitality from enemies and redirects it to themselves, their pet, or their allies
  • Has defensive shielding options that offset the health cost of resource mechanics
  • Has a variety of CC options including fears, snares, and the ability to drop out of a fight entirely
  • One of the strongest solo classes in the roster
What the devs say is changing

The Necromancer's fantasy isn't changing. The update is about delivering more of it by pulling away from generic caster utility and expanding on the themes it already owns. The biggest shifts are in spell design; life and death manipulation, corruption, and the bond with their undead minion are all getting more depth and mechanical expression.

Generic arcane utility that could belong to any caster is going away. The pet system is also expanding with more progression, collectable pet types, and a more distinct interaction tempo that separates the Necromancer from the Elementalist despite both being pet-heavy classes.

Health and resource management are getting a risk/reward mechanic that makes the red line feel like a genuine playstyle rather than a hazard to avoid.

More: Unique utility tied to death, corruption, and life drain; Pet collection and expanded pet progression; Resource tap and transfer abilities; Risk/reward mechanics around health and resource management

Less: Generic magical utilities that don't fit the death theme

Solo: Official class page: 'One of the strongest solo classes in the roster'; undead pet holds the front line.

Group: DoTs, life-tap, pet; Locate Corpse at L1 (0.30.2.3).

strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Necromancer lacks (Magic DPS, Pet, Utility).

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Intelligent strategy

Your Intelligence is increased by 5%. Active: Become intelligent, increasing your Intelligence by 50% for 15 seconds.

Intelligent: +5% INT and a 15-second +50% INT burst.

Alternatives: Tough, Corruption Resistant

Minor Combat: Alterer strategy

Your Alteration skill starts at 10. Your Alteration skill increases 25% faster. ''Alteration spells heal and enhance allies, and hinder enemies.''

Alteration (51) and Conjuration (41) are the two biggest Necromancer schools on the wiki.

Alternatives: Conjurer, Channeler

Major Non-Combat: Readiness strategy

You start with an available Cantrip slot, which can be assigned to a memorized spell to retain it after death.

Readiness gives a Cantrip slot from level 1, so one memorized spell survives death.

Alternatives: Forage, Infravision, Ultravision

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack
