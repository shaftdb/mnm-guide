Bonus-point advice (wiki)

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Wisdom is a Druid’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Wisdom is directly tied to your healing spells effectiveness and total mana. After that, prioritize Charisma. It helps with the Druid's charm line of spells, along with a few other spell lines. Finally, put a point into Dexterity if you have any left. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Goblin: +1 Dexterity, +6 Wisdom, and +3 Charisma Halfling: +3 Dexterity, +3 Wisdom, and +4 Charisma Human: +2 Dexterity, +7 Wisdom, and +1 Charisma Wood Elf: +1 Dexterity, +7 Wisdom, and +2 Charisma

strategy Race picks this guide suggests: Deep Gnome, Goblin, Halfling, Human, Wood Elf (large races left out).

Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Specializes in nature magic, ranging from damage‑over‑time and elemental nukes to healing‑over‑time and snares
  • Wields teleports, evacuates, damage shields, regeneration, and high‑impact nukes as signature tools
  • Functions as a primary healer with strong direct‑damage capabilities at the cost of a lighter buff suite than the Shaman
  • Excels at solo play through control over animals and self‑sufficiency
  • One of the strongest solo classes
What the devs say is changing

The Druid's role isn't changing. They remain a primary healer with genuine damage capabilities and strong solo utility.

The update focuses on depth over breadth. Key spell lines are getting better upgrades and scaling, giving the class more to work with in the areas it already owns. In exchange, some of the more generic utility is going away, particularly where it overlaps with other priest classes.

More: Depth in core nature magic lines; Upgrades and scaling for existing spells; Unique utilities that feel distinctly Druid

Less: Elemental spells without a nature theme; Spells shared with the Ranger

Solo: Official class page: 'One of the strongest solo classes'; excels solo through control over animals and self-sufficiency.

Group: Secondary healer, damage, ports, Seasons system (deep dive).

strategy Duo partners: Shadow Knight, Fighter, Paladin, Necromancer — chosen to cover what Druid lacks (Healer, Magic DPS, Support).
