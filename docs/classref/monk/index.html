Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Utilizes bare fists, kicks, fist weapons, and staves in combat
  • Can feign death as a cornerstone pulling and escape tool
  • Can serve as an avoidance-based off-tank via dodges and blocks; limited in taunt toolkit
  • Has stances that enhance either damage, avoidance, or speed, with shared cooldowns that create meaningful combat choices
  • Has disciplines that provide powerful, long-cooldown bursts of offense or near-invulnerability
  • Has physical debuffs to impair melee and caster opponents
  • Capable of soloing due to their self-reliance and can mend their own wounds
What the devs say is changing

The biggest change involves the Monk’s combat choices. Their current toolkit is lean in a way that flattens moment-to-moment gameplay, so through expansion of the shared cooldown system, players will be able to make genuine decisions during a fight rather than settling into a fixed rotation. Ability scaling and upgrades are also being fleshed out across the full level range.

The other change is thematic. The elemental theme in their current abilities is being replaced with lore and aesthetics that belong specifically to the Monk. The class earns its identity through what it does, not through buffs it hands to others, and the ability design is being brought in line with that.

More: Meaningful combat choices through expanded shared cooldowns; Depth, scaling, and upgrades across the full level range; Monk-specific lore and thematic identity in ability design; Tools to assist with scouting and pulling; Self-reliance

Less: Generic elemental themes; Buffs for other players

Solo: Official class page: capable of soloing thanks to self-reliance and self-healing. Feign Death helps pulling.

Group: Melee damage, pulling with Feign Death (deep dive).

strategy Duo partners: Fighter, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Shaman — chosen to cover what Monk lacks (Melee DPS, Puller).

Stat points

fact Class modifier: +1 STR, +3 DEX, +4 AGI (wiki Monk Creation Guide). Every listed race gets 10 bonus points at creation (wiki race pages).

fact Mana: No spell on the wiki Monk list has a numeric mana cost.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Puller (default) strategy

Monks pull with feign death and can avoidance off-tank (official). AGI mitigation and STA keep you alive on the pull; DEX for hit rate.

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Agile strategy

Your Agility is increased by 5%. Active: Increases your Agility by 50% for 15 seconds.

Agile: +5% AGI and a 15-second +50% AGI burst for avoidance.

Alternatives: Dexterous, Strong

Minor Combat: Brawler strategy

Your Dual Wield skill starts at 20. Your Dual Wield skill increases 25% faster.

Brawler (Monk only) starts Dual Wield at 20 and raises it 25% faster.

Alternatives: Defender, Warrior

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Monks may take it. Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Medic strategy

Your Bind Wound skill starts at 25 and your Survival skill starts at 5. Your Bind Wound skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Survival Satchel and 20 Trainee's Cloth Bandages.

Medic for bandages between pulls; Monks can mend their own wounds (official) but Bind Wound still helps early. Miner for coin.

Alternatives: Miner, Survivalist

Tradeskills strategy

  • Mining: Gathering for coin; pairs with the Miner trait (this Codex's default set).
  • Smelting: Turns ore into sellable bars (separate skill on the wiki).
  • Lumberjacking: Second gathering skill for coin, part of the default set.
  • Bind Wound: Bandaging between pulls cuts downtime for classes without strong self-heals.

Gathering defaults plus Bind Wound. Smithing stays off. STRATEGY.
