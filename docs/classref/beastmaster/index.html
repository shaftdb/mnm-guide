Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Wields two-handed weapons or double-fisted claws
  • Maintains one persistent animal companion that fights alongside them in melee combat
  • Companion must be healed, cared for, and revived if it dies in combat
  • Uses a taming mechanic to acquire companions that is distinct from summoning
  • Temporarily calls upon hawks, rats, and other creatures in specific abilities
  • Has abilities that can buff them, their companion, and party members
  • Has bestial-themed stances for different combat (damage, defense) and utility modes (speed, stealth, resistance)
  • Has survivalist abilities including self and companion heals
What the devs say is changing

The Beastmaster is leaving behind its roots as a Shaman hybrid. The spirit world connection, shamanistic abilities, and swarm pets are going away entirely. What replaces them is a more grounded identity as a primal melee fighter, whose power comes from physical mastery and their bond with animals, not magical tradition.

More: Two-handed weapons and double-fisted claws as weapon options; Animal-themed abilities and contextual creatures throughout the toolkit; Primal stances that buff the Beastmaster, their companion, and the party; Beastmaster and companion-specific survival abilities

Less: Shared abilities with Shamans; Traditional hybrid role, instead becoming a unique class; Swarm pet mechanics

Solo: Pet tanks with Guardian Spirit for solo/small group (deep dive).

Group: Melee + pet damage; pet roles DPS / Tank / Balanced.

strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Beastmaster lacks (Melee DPS, Pet).
