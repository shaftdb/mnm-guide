Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Physical ranged DPS with no magic
  • Can fire at a reduced speed while moving
  • Has access to specialty arrowheads, including explosive tips, entangling nets, concealing smoke, and more (key abilities require specific arrowheads, so what you carry matters)
  • Utilizes tinkered traps including caltrops, nets, and bombs; their tools feel crafted and mechanical, rather than foraged like a Ranger
  • Can fire a quick volley while repositioning
  • Can leap backward when enemies close in
  • Can temporarily conceal themselves to empower their first shot upon reemerging
What the devs say is changing

Archer updates are primarily thematic. We’re not overhauling their core abilities, but rather tightening up their focus. You can expect the same ranged DPS role with a stronger emphasis on what makes the class distinct: power from preparation and positioning.

More: Physical skill and technique as the primary source of power; Loadout-driven gameplay using specialized arrows and traps; Emphasis on planning, positioning, and resource management

Less: Magic and elemental abilities; Passive or automatic damage

Solo: Wiki: archers 'find it difficult to adventure alone' at times; deep dive adds traps, stances and backward leap for kiting.

Group: Ranged damage with situational utility (traps, debuffs).

strategy Duo partners: Fighter, Shadow Knight, Paladin, Shaman — chosen to cover what Archer lacks (Ranged DPS).

Stat points

fact Class modifier: +2 STR, +4 DEX, +2 AGI (wiki Archer Creation Guide). Every listed race gets 10 bonus points at creation (wiki race pages).

fact Mana: Your mana stat is whichever of INT or WIS is higher (wiki Statistics); 1 of 60 wiki Archer spells cost mana. Deep Elf Archer: INT 19, WIS 17, so your mana stat is INT.Goblin Archer: INT 17, WIS 16, so your mana stat is INT.Halfling Archer: INT 14, WIS 20, so your mana stat is WIS.Human Archer: INT 16, WIS 16, so your mana stat is INT or WIS (tied at creation).Wood Elf Archer: INT 15, WIS 17, so your mana stat is WIS.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Damage (default) strategy

+4 DEX · +4 STR · +2 STA

The official page calls the Archer physical ranged DPS with no magic. DEX raises hit rate and procs and STR adds ranged weapon damage (wiki Statistics), so most points go there; 2 STA as a cushion.

Safer kiter strategy

+4 DEX · +3 AGI · +3 STA

Archers fire while moving and leap back when enemies close in (official). AGI mitigation and STA health help when a mob does reach you.

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Dexterous strategy

Your Dexterity is increased by 5%. Active: Become dexterous, increasing your Dexterity by 50% for 15 seconds.

Dexterous: +5% DEX and a 15-second +50% DEX burst, your main hit-rate stat. Strong for more damage per hit.

Alternatives: Strong, Agile

Minor Combat: Bowman strategy

Your Archery skill starts at 5. Your Archery skill increases 25% faster. You start with a Trainee's Longbow, a Trainee's Hip Quiver, and 1000 Trainee's Arrows.

Bowman starts Archery at 5, raises it 25% faster and gives a Trainee's Longbow, Hip Quiver and 1000 Trainee's Arrows, a big head start for your main weapon.

Alternatives: Knife Thrower, Defender

Major Non-Combat: Forage strategy

Search your surroundings, attempting to gather food, drink, and other supplies.

Forage finds food and drink in the field, so fewer vendor runs. Archers may take it (Template:Traits). Infravision or Ultravision only if your race may take it (see the race note).

For a Halfling: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

For a Wood Elf: Infravision (you already have Forage as a racial).

Alternatives: Infravision, Ultravision, Slow Metabolism

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

    Tradeskills strategy

    • Mining: Gathering for coin; pairs with the Miner trait (this Codex's default set).
    • Smelting: Turns ore into sellable bars (separate skill on the wiki).
    • Lumberjacking: Second gathering skill for coin, part of the default set.
    • Fletching: Make your own arrows instead of buying them.

    Archer key abilities need specific arrowheads (official page), so Fletching is added to the gathering defaults. Smithing stays off. STRATEGY.
