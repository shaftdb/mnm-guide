Hotbar planner

Plan your bars for the Class, Race and Levels picked in the top bar. Drag a spell or ability onto a slot, or click/tap it and then a slot. Keyboard: Enter picks up, arrow keys move between slots, Enter places, Delete clears, Esc cancels. fact Spells and abilities come from your class's wiki spell list, gated by level. strategy Suggested layouts are rules of thumb, not tested rotations.

How M&M bars work (sourced vs unverified)
  • fact Ability bar: 10 slots for memorized spells and abilities, used with keys 1 to 0. Melee abilities (Innates tab) and spells both have to be memorized here. (Official New Player Guide, Official Wizard deep dive, Wiki: Meming your Spell/Abilities)
  • fact Hotbar: keys Shift+1 to 0, minus and equals (12 buttons). It holds skills from the Skills tab, macros and clicky items, not memorized spells. (Official New Player Guide)
  • fact Aura bar: keys Ctrl+1 to 0. Hide and Sneak sit on the Aura bar and need no memorization; other trait abilities are skills that go on the HotBar. (Official New Player Guide, Patch 0.20.25.0)
  • fact Your spellbook has to be in a belt slot (3 belt slots) to scribe or memorize, but not to cast. (Official New Player Guide)
  • unverified Hotbar pages: players report 12 slots shown at a time with switchable hotbar pages, and only one hotbar on screen. The number of pages and the page-switch keys are not documented, so the page count here is a setting. (Forum: "My UI Wishlist", Forum: "Ability Bar Re-shape?")
  • unverified Aura bar size: 10 keys are bound (Ctrl+1 to 0); how many aura slots the bar shows is not documented. (Official New Player Guide)
  • unverified Cantrips: you gain cantrip slots that keep a memorized spell on death; how many, and at which levels, is not documented, so this planner does not model them. (Patch 0.20.25.0)

Gear slots: names from the wiki Equipment By Slot list and its item slot codes fact; 3 belt slots from the official guide fact; two each of Ear, Wrist and Finger is an assumption unverified.

    Your spells and abilities

    Macro button Macros run slash commands (see /help in game); they go on the hotbar.

    Character sheet

    Pick gear from the Item database for each slot (filtered to your class and slot). Totals use only the item stats on the wiki item pages fact plus the base stats of the build you pick: your race's starting stats for this class plus that build's bonus points (wiki race pages; builds are this Codex's strategy). Base resists, level-up stat gains and caps are not sourced, so they are not added unverified.
