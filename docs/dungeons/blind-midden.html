Getting in fact

  • fact Dungeon just off Ail'Vorith, the Deep Elf starting zone (wiki). It has a main entrance and a back entrance (wiki NPC locations).

Directions to key camps fact

  • Gorgeous: Top floor
  • Flavic the Fisher: Top floor
  • Mucky Morgurah: Top floor
  • Forager Davra: Top floor
  • Sethrine the Unblinking: Top floor
  • Mother Gummer: Top floor, in the watery hallways where the gelmothers spawn
  • Underboss Vekrizin: Top floor
  • Cedrius the Cutter: Top floor
  • Blacksmith Jerictus: Top floor, forge
  • Wikket Two-Wands: Top floor, prisons
  • Reviver Vlarek: Top floor, in the room with the sentient sewage.
  • Elixa the Experimentalist: Basement, main entrance. Straight forward, follow water to a room full of science equipment
  • Enthraller Gnorah: Basement, main entrance. First room on the right
  • Gurm the Half-Woken: Basement, main entrance. Take left entrance to upper levels towards Gnuttoc. First room up ramp
  • Dakkin the Diabolical: Basement, main entrance. Up ramp towards Gnuttoc in antechamber that merges the two paths
  • Knuttoc Grobbins: Basement
  • Flashfreeze Fraegra: Basement, main entrance. Dungeons area right after Enthraller Gnorah
  • Biomass: Basement, main entrance. Left turn at dungeons area where Flashfreeze Fraegra is
  • Tormentor Tizzix: Basement, torture room around corner from Biomass
  • Gelmother Jiggol: Basement, back entrance. Straight ahead of the back entrance room.
  • Pragrok the Pickaxe: Basement, back entrance. Take a right out of back entrance room, first room on right. First ogre on the left in the little miner room.
  • Willie the Watcher: Basement, back entrance. Take a right out of back entrance room, first room on right. Patrols the raised path.
  • Forager Frandrah: Basement, back entrance. Take a right out of back entrance room, first room on right. Middle of the 2 harvesters in the corner.
  • Ferg the Filthy: Basement, back entrance. Hallway that connects Red Hands Ellison and Andrew the Framed
  • Sarveth the Unheard: On the upper level, at the end of the main hallway.
