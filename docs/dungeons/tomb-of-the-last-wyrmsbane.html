Special info

  • fact Map key lists pit traps at B, K and N, with recovery points C (for B) and L (for K) (wiki map legend).
  • fact Hidden passage O leads to the Chapel Room (4); hidden zone line M to Ancient Crypt sits above a hallway (wiki map legend).
  • fact Bone Construct (room 5): patch 0.29.0.0 made him despawn if he leaves his arena; 0.29.0.1 says his gate no longer locks until his corpse rots, and all Bone Construct rewards require level 49 to equip.
  • fact I leads to a basement filled with Deep Beetles (wiki map legend).

Pathing and pull tips strategy

  • strategy Learn where the pit traps are before you pull through hallways; a fall splits the group.
  • strategy Bone Construct loot needs level 49, so low-level groups gain nothing from killing him.
