Special info

  • fact A hidden Ashira sanctuary; wiki level 10–35. Monster types: Ashira, jackals, goblins (wiki).
  • fact The wiki map key splits the zone into an Ashira side (30 numbered NPCs, 1–30 on the schematic) and a Bloodynose side (General Overlord Bloodynose and the Bloodynose hag, trainer and muralist, B1–B4 on the schematic).
  • fact Higher named on the wiki: Animist Wafi (~31–33), Ashir the Protector (25), General Overlord Bloodynose (20+), an ashira alchemist (20+).

Pathing and pull tips strategy

  • strategy Lower named (Roa the Huntress 12, Ayip 13, Hadhraman 17) suit a group in the teens; save Ashir and Animist Wafi for the 25–35 end.
  • strategy Many Ashira-side NPCs are merchants and trainers; con-check before attacking, since killing them may cost faction (faction effects not sourced).
