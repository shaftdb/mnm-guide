Special info

  • fact Zone respawn timer: 8 minutes (wiki).
  • fact False-floor pit trap A drops you among skeletons; C is the recovery from A. Pit trap D drops you into Caves of Irem. B is a ladder up to the Researcher Harseal area (wiki map legend).

Pathing and pull tips strategy

  • strategy With an 8-minute respawn, clear toward the named you want and don't linger.
  • strategy Avoid false floor D unless you mean to go to Caves of Irem.
