Getting in fact

  • fact Entrance: second-to-last building along the river in Sungreet Strand. Enter the courtyard, turn around to face the exit, click the plain wall right of the gate to reveal a secret entrance (wiki).
  • fact The Grain Cellar Key is required for the many gated doors inside (wiki). Zakariah Shriki (31) drops a Grain Cellar Key (wiki NPC table).
