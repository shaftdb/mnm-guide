Special info

  • fact Listed in the wiki's Dungeons category; wiki level 10–30.
  • fact Monster types: orcs, undead, ratmen, bats, spiders, wolves (wiki).
  • fact Highest-level named with a level on the wiki: Commander Rursk (15), Gurowl, The Beast (14), a Fellstone ritualist (14). Several loot-dropping named (a swarm leader, a Skri'Var hoarder, a frenzied ratman, an orcish tactician) have no level on the wiki.

Pathing and pull tips strategy

  • strategy The listed named sit at 11–15, the low end of the 10–30 range, so a duo can try them; check con on the unlevelled named first.
  • strategy With orcs, ratmen, undead and spiders mixed together, expect mixed-type adds; pull to a cleared spot.
