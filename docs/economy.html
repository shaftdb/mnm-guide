Economy & trade routes

Spell scroll pricing

  • Sep 30 (0.30.2.1): each 5-level range has a set total cost that's the same for every class. Earlier scrolls in a range cost less than later ones. Shared scrolls like Gate have one low price.
  • Oct 3 (0.30.2.3): cheaper for classes that have very few scrolls.
  • Oct 7 (0.30.2.5): a smoother ramp from 1–60 and a lower total. The devs are "looking into giving some coin back" for scrolls bought before the change.

Income

See Farming. Evershade Weald has the highest income per hour of the starting cities, according to the devs (Oct 4).

Trade routes

unconfirmed Neither the wiki nor the patch notes document cross-city price differences. Shadowless Deep Elves "are exploited by the merchants in the foreign quarter" in Ail'Vorith (Oct 4 patch note), so prices clearly vary by race and faction. Any specific arbitrage route would be a guess, so this section stays empty until someone measures one in game.

Gather-and-hand-off pipeline strategy

One player gathers and smelts; a crafter friend or guildmate finishes gear with those mats. Self-sufficient, no auction needed.

  • Hand-in list and exact craft quantities: Mats to give guild crafters
  • Node routes: Gathering route (Shaman)
  • Copper is player-gated (vendors do not sell copper ore — Blacksmithing wiki). Bars are the main currency you bring the guild.
