Alchemy skill-up guide

Alchemy is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Alchemy (edited 2026-10-09).

RolePotions, Serums, Elixirs & Powders
TrainerAlchemist Vizyl, Alchemist Maverak
LocationNight Harbor (Blackfeather Bazaar), Underdocks (Western district)
Primary ToolMortar and Pestle
Crafting StationCauldron

Alchemy is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It focuses on breaking down herbs collected from Herbalism and reagents to brew powerful temporary buffs, utilities, and crafting materials.

  • 💡 Crafting Note: To save space, standard Size/Weight columns have been removed. Unless otherwise noted, Powders are Weight 0.1 / Size TINY, and Potions/Serums are Weight 1.0 / Size SMALL. Browse by skill tier using the tabs below, or click the quick jump links inside each tab to skip to specific level brackets.

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
