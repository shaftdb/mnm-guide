Fishing skill-up guide

Fishing is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Fishing (edited 2026-10-14).

RoleCatching Fish, Crabs & Reagents
TrainersFisherman Abduh, Fisherman Dren, Expert Fisherman
LocationsNight Harbor, Evershade Weald, Underdocks
Primary ToolFishing Pole
Required StationFishable Water Source

Fishing is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It allows players to harvest seafood and aquatic reagents from various bodies of water across the world. Fishing pairs exceptionally well with Cooking, allowing you to turn your catches into substantial meals (e.g., combining Whitefish with Butter to make Pan Fried Whitefish, which lasts considerably longer than standard rations).

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
