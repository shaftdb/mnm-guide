Smelting skill-up guide

Smelting is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Smelting (edited 2026-10-07).

RoleMetal Refining & Alloys
TrainerSmelter Jadid, Jedara Kilnwright
LocationNight Harbor (Night Market), Underdocks (Forge Row)
Primary ToolIngot Molds
Crafting StationForge

Smelting is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It is the foundational process of refining raw ores mined from the earth—or recycling metal scraps—into usable metal bars for Blacksmithing, Jewelcrafting, and other crafts.

  • ⚠️ Crafting Note: All smelting recipes listed below must be performed at a Forge. Click the column headers to sort the data!

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
