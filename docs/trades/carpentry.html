Carpentry skill-up guide

Carpentry is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Carpentry (edited 2026-10-10).

RoleFurniture, Structures & Wood Goods
TrainerCarpenter Quais
LocationNight Harbor (Shipyard)
WorkstationCarpentry Table

Carpentry is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It utilizes components and materials sourced from Woodworking, Blacksmithing, and Alchemy.
