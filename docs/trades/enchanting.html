Enchanting skill-up guide

Enchanting is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Enchanting (edited 2026-10-13).

RoleEquipment Scrolls, Bars & Scraps
TrainersEnchanter Haffiz, Wyrel
LocationsNight Harbor, Faelindral
Crafting StationTome of Enchantment Enchanting Station

Enchanting is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It relies upon resources harvested from Disenchanting magic items and metal bars created via Smelting. Enchanted bars are utilized by Jewelcrafting, while reagents collected from disenchanting are used to create Enchanting Scrolls—consumable, tradable items that permanently add stat boosts to player equipment.

  • 💡 Crafting Note: Browse by enchantment tier using the tabs below. Jump to specific level brackets within each tier using the quick navigation links.

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
