Jewelcrafting (commonly referred to as JC) is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It allows players to unlock the hidden power in precious stones by crafting powerful rings, earrings, and necklaces. Jewelcrafters accrue many of the gems they need from mineral veins, making it heavily synergistic with the Mining gathering profession. Over time, jewelcrafting can also become a lucrative source of income.

  • 💡 Quick Start Note: If you chose to start with the minor non-combat trait for jewelcrafting during character creation, you do not need to locate these NPCs—you are ready to craft immediately!
