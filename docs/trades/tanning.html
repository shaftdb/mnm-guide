Tanning skill-up guide

Tanning is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Tanning (edited 2026-09-20).

RolePelt Refining & Canvas
TrainerTanner Burhan
LocationsNight Harbor, Faelindral, Keeper's Bight, Underdocks
Crafting StationTanning Bucket

Tanning is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It is a refinement profession utilized to convert raw animal pelts into usable canvas materials for the Leatherworking tradeskill.

  • 💡 Crafting Tip: When converting pelts into canvas, you must place them into the Tanning Bucket one at a time before pressing craft. The output quantity of canvas is based on the quality of the pelt. Note that obtaining these pelts directly from beasts currently does not require a specialized skinning skill!

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
