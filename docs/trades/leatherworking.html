Leatherworking skill-up guide

Leatherworking is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Leatherworking (edited 2026-10-05).

RoleLeather Armor, Containers & Mount Gear
TrainersLeatherworker Haiat, Leatherworker Keiros
LocationNight Harbor (The Tannery), Underdocks
Primary ToolsLeatherworking Kit Seam Ripper
Crafting StationLeatherworking Station

Leatherworking is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It generally relies upon resources dropped by mobs or harvested from the world. Several recipes will also require components crafted by Tanning, Tailoring, and Enchanting.

  • 💡 Progression Tip: It is highly recommended to purchase a Seam Ripper. When combined with Tattered Rawhide armor in your kit, it produces Rawhide Scraps. This process continues into higher-level leather armors and is an excellent way to acquire extra materials for leveling up.
  • 💡 Crafting Note: To save space, the "Crafting Bench" column has been removed. Recipes with 6 or fewer ingredients can be crafted in a Leatherworking Kit. Recipes with 7+ ingredients MUST be crafted at a Leatherworking Station. Use the tabbed categories below to browse recipes by item tier.

Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first.
