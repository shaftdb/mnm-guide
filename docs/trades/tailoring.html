Tailoring skill-up guide

Tailoring is not in your selected tradeskills. Add it with the Trades picker in the top bar.

fact From the wiki page Skill Tailoring (edited 2026-10-10).

RoleCloth Armor, Bags & Components
TrainersNoura the Tailor, Flerri Dawntender, Yasmin Selvar
LocationNight Harbor, Evershade Weald, Underdocks
Primary ToolsTailoring Kit Seam Ripper
Crafting StationLoom

Tailoring is one of the many tradeskills in Monsters & Memories. It generally relies upon resources dropped by mobs or harvested from the world. Several recipes will also require components crafted by Blacksmithing.

  • 💡 Progression Tip: It is highly recommended to find a Tailoring Vendor such as Master Tailor Sansha and purchase a Seam Ripper. When combined with Tattered Cloth armor in your kit, it produces Cloth Scraps. This process continues into higher-level cloth armors and is an excellent way to acquire extra materials for leveling up.
  • 💡 Crafting Note: To save space, the "Crafting Bench" column has been removed. Recipes with 6 or fewer ingredients can be crafted in a Tailoring Kit. Recipes with 7+ ingredients MUST be crafted at a Loom. Use the tabbed categories below to browse recipes by item tier.

strategy How this is mapped: on this page every zone shows its wiki level so you can judge safety; the per-level guidance on the Tradeskills and Leveling pages uses your character level range to pick zones: a zone counts as safe when its low level on the wiki zone page is at or below the top of your range. Zones with no level on the wiki are marked zone level unverified and never counted as safe. fact Node and recipe skill numbers (min skill to attempt, trivial = skill-ups stop) are copied from the wiki tradeskill pages. Character level and tradeskill level are separate; wide zones like "1–50" still have dangerous corners, so /consider first. Costs: the wiki and merchant data list no prices, so costs are not sourced.
