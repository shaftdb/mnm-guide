My Shaman — level 12 → next steps

Healer, slower and buffer. Chain armor, blunt/piercing weapons, WIS first. Current focus: slow + DoT soloing to 16, then the big level-16 spell batch.

Right now (level 12)

  • Buy your level 12 spells: Consume, Consuming Pact, Omen of Enfeeblement, Root, Cure Disease, Dispel Magic, Enduring Breath. table
  • Make sure you have the level 8 batch. Pact of Renewal moved to level 8 in patch 0.30.2.1. Ghostly Blight, Pact of Haste, Spirit Mending, Speed of the Wild are also level 8.
  • Solo loop: Omen of Lethargy → Ghostly Blight + Malady → Pact of Renewal on yourself → melee or wait. Use Consume only when safe (45 s cooldown).

Where to level 12–16

  • Night Harbor: wiki lists diseased bats (10), cracked/diseased beetles (12–14) and decrepit→cackling skeletons (12–15).
  • Infested Crypt (wiki 8–16) or Tel Ekir (wiki 10–35).
  • From an Underdocks start: wererats (10–12).
  • Full Shaman route →

Level 16 is a big upgrade

  • Greater Omen of Lethargy (20% slow), Greater Pact of Renewal, Greater Pact of Haste, Sickness, Greater Ghostly Blight
  • Invisibility, Omen of Susceptibility, Berserker Rage / Tribal Rage (needs a Ritual Talisman, sold by a shaman scribe in the Seekers of the Lost Eye)
  • Save coin for these. Scroll prices were cut on Oct 7.

Gear for 11–20

  • Shaman easy-BiS by level band →
  • All-class jewelry from early nameds, like Spitemaw's Ring (Dhajr Spitemaw, Shaded Dunes, 8+).
  • Chain: the Bronze/Copper chain sets are craftable. See crafted sets.

What changed recently (game patches)

  • Oct 11, 0.30.2.7 and 0.30.2.8: fixes for economic exploits with mispriced tradeskill items. Night Harbor is one zone again.
  • Oct 7, 0.30.2.5: spell scrolls are cheaper with a smoother ramp across 1–60. The devs are "looking into giving some coin back" to people who bought scrolls before the cut. Cureberries are now foraged by Druids and Rangers instead of summoned.
  • Oct 4, 0.30.2.4: the devs say all four starting cities give similar income per hour, with Evershade Weald slightly ahead. They also say tradeskills are expensive to start.
  • Sep 30, 0.30.2.1 (release): Shaman Pact of Renewal ranks now come 4 levels earlier (starting at 8) and heal faster. Shaman invisibility no longer ends right after you cast it. Tribal Spirit, Speed of the Pack and Communion of Regeneration can be cast on players outside your group.
