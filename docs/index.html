Tips

fact Official class page — defining traits:

  • Fights exclusively with melee weapons, utilizing a shield for tanking and dual-wield or two‑handed weapons for damage
  • Has a strong toolkit of physical debuffs and preventative counters to incoming damage
  • Operates on a shared cooldown pool that forces meaningful choices in each combat cycle, with stances that shift the kit toward offense, defense, or control
  • Performs best against melee opponents and gains additional value when defending due to retaliatory mechanics
  • Possesses the strongest aggro‑control tools in the roster, enabling unmatched threat generation and stability
What the devs say is changing

The Fighter is being reshaped into a dedicated tank rather than one that also competes on damage. They’ll gain a broader aggro and survival toolkit, more ways to counter incoming damage, and a stronger retaliation suite. Overall damage output comes down in exchange.

More: Taunts and threat-management tools; Tanking problem solvers; Retaliation abilities

Less: Melee DPS, but still competitive

Solo: Wiki: most comfortable in a group.

Group: Premier tank: stances, Retaliation/Vengeance (deep dive). Kick & Shield Bash share a cooldown (0.30.2.1).

strategy Duo partners: Cleric, Shaman, Druid — chosen to cover what Fighter lacks (Tank, Melee DPS).

What changed recently (game patches)

  • Oct 15, 0.30.2.9 (Patch 12 after reversion): live servers rolled back from 0.30.3.2. Coin drops across many NPC types are rebalanced, some herb spawn restrictions are fixed, and Ranger Recuperate gets a fix for a bug from that reversion.
  • Oct 14, 0.30.3.2 (briefly live, then reverted): missing house crates restored for good and the scroll overcharge refund lands in your bank. Cleric HP-buff heals no longer scale with Wisdom, Ranger Recuperate is out-of-combat only, and common mobs drop more sellable loot. Woodworking caps at 300 at any level and failed crafts return some materials. Heavy server optimizations were the likely rollback trigger.
  • Oct 11, 0.30.2.7 and 0.30.2.8: fixes for economic exploits with mispriced tradeskill items. Night Harbor is one zone again.
  • Oct 7, 0.30.2.5: spell scrolls are cheaper with a smoother ramp across 1–60. The devs are "looking into giving some coin back" to people who bought scrolls before the cut. Cureberries are now foraged by Druids and Rangers instead of summoned.
  • Oct 4, 0.30.2.4: the devs say all four starting cities give similar income per hour, with Evershade Weald slightly ahead. They also say tradeskills are expensive to start.
  • Sep 30, 0.30.2.1 (release): Shaman Pact of Renewal ranks now come 4 levels earlier (starting at 8) and heal faster. Shaman invisibility no longer ends right after you cast it. Tribal Spirit, Speed of the Pack and Communion of Regeneration can be cast on players outside your group.
