Brewsham (Human Shaman)

Healer, slower and buffer. Chain armor, blunt/piercing weapons, WIS first. Profession: gather only (Mining / Lumberjacking / Smelting) — feed guild crafters. Pick a level band in the header to load Quick info for that range.

Quick info · 11–15

Default band — change anytime in the header selector.

Where to level

  • Night Harbor: diseased bats (10), cracked/diseased beetles (12–14), decrepit→cackling skeletons (12–15). Map: Night Harbor.
  • Infested Crypt (8–16): Infested Crypt.
  • Tel Ekir (10–35): Tel Ekir.
  • Underdocks wererats (10–12): Underdocks.
  • Ancient Crypt (12–22): skeletal guard/adept/inquisitor ~12–15 — Ancient Crypt.
  • Tomb of the Last Wyrmsbane (3–15): Wyrmsbane crusader 14 — Wyrmsbane.
  • Full camp tables: Shaman leveling 11–15 · all-class 11–15.

Spells to buy

  • Confirm L12: Consume, Omen of Enfeeblement, Root; nice Cure Disease, Dispel Magic; Consuming Pact later (healing reduced 2 min).
  • Confirm L8 still scribed (Pact of Renewal, Ghostly Blight, Pact of Haste, Spirit Mending, Speed of the Wild).
  • Next big batch at 16 — save coin. Table: Spells.

Gear & guild crafts

  • Band 11–15 on Shaman easy BiS.
  • Ask guild for Copper Chain (full set ≈ 108× Copper Bar + 24× Rawhide Strap / 48 scraps), Copper Mace (7 bars + Cracked Mace Mold), kite/tower shield, Jade copper jewelry — exact quantities.
  • Named/all-class jewelry (e.g. Spitemaw's Ring, Shaded Dunes 8+).

Gathering (Speed of the Wild)

  • Outdoors: Speed of the Wild between nodes. Carry Mining Pick + Lumberjack Axe.
  • Mining: Copper (NH gates, Shaded Dunes, Evershade, Keeper's Bight); Limestone ≥35 (Dunes cliffs toward Tel Ekir, Fallen Pass, Sungreet).
  • Lumber: Wood/Rich Wood same zones; Fine Wood at Sungreet / Keeper's Bight when skill allows.
  • Smelt Copper (and Tin when found) at NH Night Market forge. Details: Gathering.

Quests

  • Shaman quests · quests overlapping 10–30 on that page.
  • Aspect of the Bear quest: unconfirmed (deep dive vs wiki Research L28).

Drops / mats to keep

  • Copper/Tin bars, Wood, Resin, Limestone, LQ pelts, Rawhide scraps, Jade/Emerald, corroded bronze, tattered rawhide.
  • Do not vendor copper — vendors don't sell copper ore (BS wiki).

Tips

  • Solo: Omen of Lethargy → Ghostly Blight + Malady → Pact of Renewal → melee; Root if bad; Consume only safe (playbook). strategy
  • Gather-only profession — guild crafts for free with your mats.
  • Push toward 16 for Greater slow/HoT/haste and Invisibility.

What changed recently (game patches)

  • Oct 11, 0.30.2.7 and 0.30.2.8: fixes for economic exploits with mispriced tradeskill items. Night Harbor is one zone again.
  • Oct 7, 0.30.2.5: spell scrolls are cheaper with a smoother ramp across 1–60. The devs are "looking into giving some coin back" to people who bought scrolls before the cut. Cureberries are now foraged by Druids and Rangers instead of summoned.
  • Oct 4, 0.30.2.4: the devs say all four starting cities give similar income per hour, with Evershade Weald slightly ahead. They also say tradeskills are expensive to start.
  • Sep 30, 0.30.2.1 (release): Shaman Pact of Renewal ranks now come 4 levels earlier (starting at 8) and heal faster. Shaman invisibility no longer ends right after you cast it. Tribal Spirit, Speed of the Pack and Communion of Regeneration can be cast on players outside your group.
