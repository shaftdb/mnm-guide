Oct 18, 2026Leveling follows your start city (Ail'Vorith route): the early leveling path is now built from one data file per start city. Quick info by level, the Leveling tabs, the New-character walkthrough, class leveling pages and class quick references all show the route for the city picked in Start, and change when you change it. None of them fall back to Night Harbor without saying so: a city with no data shows "no sourced data yet". Ail'Vorith (Deep Elf) has a full route from level 1 to about 45: the newbie yard and starter quest, fungal and Varsul supply quests, House Xal'tharn parts 1 and 2, the Blind Midden, the exile caves, the Great Cavern Sea survey, goblin island, the Caves of Irem, basilisks and spiders, the Pyramid Road, Rothold and the Pyramid Ruins. It also lists every Ail'Vorith class trainer (Inquisitor: Nurvol Cereveth) with the spells to buy in each band, money camps, and a faction box that warns Night Harbor is kill-on-sight for Deep Elves and the Underdocks is the safe hub. Each starter or early quest on the route has a fold-out walkthrough covering giver, prerequisites, steps, mobs and drops, turn-in, rewards, an Optional/Recommended tag, and links to the Quest helper and the wiki. Night Harbor has partial data for levels 1–15; the Underdocks and Faelindral are placeholdersFACT: wiki Ail'Vorith, Ail'Vorith Starter Quest, House Xal'tharn quest, Citizens of Ail'Vorith, zone and NPC pages, patch notes 0.29–0.30.2. unverified (forum/Reddit): Night Harbor KOS for Deep Elves and the time needed to repair it. Gaps: Nurvol Cereveth's and Klavel's locations differ between wiki pages; the Ail'Vorith scroll seller is unknown; the Xal'tharn reward is listed two ways; Great Cavern Sea mob levels are uncertain; no route data above about 45; Night Harbor has no data from 16 up; the Underdocks and Faelindral are stubs

Large races are listed in the race table only, never recommended. Oct 18, 2026Group builder ranks every completion: pick 1 class and the builder ranks every possible pair for the other two slots. Pick 2 and it ranks every third, and with 3 picked it ranks every 4th. Each row shows its rank, a score, what it adds (strengths and money rating) and its gaps, so you can see why low options are weak. The top 5 show first, and Show all N expands the rest. Click any row to fill those slots, and the next ranking appears. Picks are still remembered in this browser and stored in the link, and old share links still loadstrategy: the score uses the same keyword-based 0–2 capability scores (they can miss or mis-tag abilities). Pairs and thirds are scored on tank, heals, DPS, CC, pulling, self-sufficiency and money. A 4th is also scored on buffs, low downtime and resurrect, plus a small bonus for strong soloists. Races shown are defaults (Human where allowed, large races skipped). Many options tie, and ties are broken by overall coverage, then by name
Oct 18, 2026Latest news: the home page shows the 5 newest Monsters & Memories news items (official updates and patch notes), and a full archive is at /news.html (News menu). Each item has a date, a 1–2 line summary taken only from the source, and a link. The data lives in content/news.json, and tools/news_add.py appends items safely: it validates fields, checks the link, removes duplicates by URL and rebuildsSeeded with official items from Sep 10 to Oct 15, 2026. Oct 18, 2026Trio plan (/trio.html): a fresh-alt trio plus optional 4th for fastest leveling, coin and self-sufficiency. Covers class and race picks, starting city, XP-range rules for a late 4th, leveling camps by band, a money camps per band table (vendor values per weight), a money plan, group habits and an optional note on how mains can help. Group builder (/groups.html): recommended trios with their best 4th (one click loads them), and a builder for 3 classes plus an optional 4th with races. Oct 17, 2026Tradeskill picker: a Trades menu sits in the top bar next to Class and Levels. You can tick several tradeskills, and the choice is remembered in this browser. By default it selects the gathering skills Mining and Lumberjacking, plus Smelting, which the wiki lists as a separate skill. Blacksmithing (smithing) is off by default but can be ticked. The Leveling page, home quick info, every class leveling page and the Tradeskills pages now show only the picked tradeskills, and the Tradeskills pages have a Show all switch. Tradeskills for your level range: for each picked skill, a panel shows the nodes and recipes in reach. It uses the wiki min and trivial skill values (FACT) and lists zones that fit your character level (strategy: a zone fits when its wiki zone level starts at or below the top of your range). It also shows where to get each material: gathering nodes, vendors, drops and scrapping. Skill-up guides: each tradeskill has a page (/trades/&lt;skill&gt;.html) with steps from skill 1 in skill brackets, the nodes or recipes to work in each bracket, and where to get the materials. They're linked from the level-range panels and the Tradeskills pages. Gear badges: gear pages, Shaman gear and home quick-info gear show a small badge such as "Tailoring 45" on items that a picked tradeskill makes or uses. A badge only appears if the item is usable by the picked class at the picked level range, and it updates when any picker changes. Also fixed: the tradeskill table extractor kept only the first column header of wiki tables, so recipe columns were mislabeledNo costs: the wiki and merchant data have no prices, so every cost reads "cost not sourced". Zones with no wiki zone level are marked "zone level unverified" and never counted as safe. Ores, Limestone and Coal are linked to veins by name only (unverified), because the Mining chart doesn't say what each vein yields. Badges taken only from an item page's "crafted" field, with no recipe table linking the item, are marked unverified (shown with "?"). No wiki data: Animal Taming, Archaeology, Astronomy, Brewing, Cartography, Farming, Fermenting, Masonry, Navigation, Pottery, Riding, Skinning, Spellcrafting, Spinning, Spycraft, Wagoneering, Wilderness. Thin data: Stone Cutting, Woodworking, Poison Making, Tinkering, Survival, Bind Wound. Fishing has no per-fish trivials. Some Alchemy, Cooking, Smelting and Fletching recipes have no trivial on the wiki
