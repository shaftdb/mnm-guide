Blind Midden

Blind Midden is a dungeon just off Ali'Vorith, the starting zone for the Deep Elf race.

Added to EA in patch 0.30.2.0 (Sep 25: "Initial zone and spawning"); still marked as in progress in 0.30.2.1.
