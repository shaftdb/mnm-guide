Shaman

New-character walkthrough for your Race and Start picks: loading… (needs JavaScript). Or open the full walkthrough.

Shamans draw power from the spirit realm. The official deep dive frames them as enhancers and debilitators: they make the group hit harder, break enemies down with slows and Omens, and keep everyone topped up with heal-over-time spells. The combat pet is gone. Spirit Wolf and Summon Companion were removed in the rework, even though the wiki description still mentions "beastial spirits".

Wiki creation advice for Shaman fact

Listed below is the recommended allocation of bonus points by race. Since Wisdom is a Shaman’s main attribute, you should focus on maximizing it whenever possible. Wisdom is directly tied to your healing spells effectiveness and total mana. After that, prioritize Stamina. It gives you extra HP and a bit of stun resistance. Finally, put a point into Dexterity if you have any left. It affects hit rate across the board and increases your proc chance. Goblin: +4 Stamina and +6 Wisdom Human: +2 Stamina, +1 Dexterity, and +7 Wisdom Ogre: +2 Dexterity and +8 Wisdom Wood Elf: +3 Stamina and +7 Wisdom

Quoted from the community wiki class page; it is advice, not a game rule.

What each stat does (wiki) fact
  • STA: Increases health and reduces chance to be dazed in combat (wiki Statistics; '1 STA = 1 HP' is marked 'need testing' there).
  • AGI: Provides damage mitigation (wiki Statistics).
  • STR: Increases melee and ranged weapon damage and shield block amounts; 1 STR = 2 weight (wiki Statistics).
  • DEX: Increases hit rate, weapon proc frequency and gives a channeling bonus to spellcasters (wiki Statistics).
  • INT: Mana pool size if INT is higher than WIS, and offensive spell damage (wiki Statistics).
  • WIS: Mana pool size if WIS is higher than INT, and healing done; 4 mana per WIS point for WIS-based classes (wiki Statistics).
  • CHA: Merchant prices and charm break rate on paper; the wiki notes CHA did not seem to affect vendor prices in beta (May 2026).

Traits

fact You pick one Major Combat, one Minor Combat, one Major Non-Combat and one Minor Non-Combat trait; your race also grants a racial ability (wiki Traits).

Major Combat: Wise strategy

Your base Wisdom is increased by 5%. Active: Become wise, increasing your Wisdom by 50% for 15 seconds.

Wise: +5% base WIS and a 15-second +50% WIS burst.

Alternatives: Tough, Cold Resistant

Minor Combat: Alterer strategy

Your Alteration skill starts at 10. Your Alteration skill increases 25% faster. ''Alteration spells heal and enhance allies, and hinder enemies.''

Alteration is by far the biggest Shaman school on the wiki (87 of 123): buffs, slows and heals over time.

Alternatives: Channeler, Conjurer

Major Non-Combat: Readiness strategy

You start with an available Cantrip slot, which can be assigned to a memorized spell to retain it after death.

Readiness gives a Cantrip slot from level 1, so one memorized spell survives death.

Alternatives: Forage, Infravision, Ultravision

Minor Non-Combat: Miner strategy

Your Mining skill starts at 25. Your Mining skill increases 5% faster. You start with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Trainee's Mining Pouch.

Miner starts Mining at 25 with a Trainee's Mining Pick and Pouch, matching the default gathering setup (Mining, Lumberjacking, Smelting) for early coin. Medic (Bind Wound 25 and bandages) is the safer pick if you expect downtime between fights. Blacksmith exists but smithing stays off by default here.

Alternatives: Medic, Lumberjack

    Your job in a group

    1. Slow first. The deep dive calls Omen of Lethargy "often the most useful thing you'll do in a hard fight." Wiki ranks: 10% at L4, Greater 20% at L16, Hex 30% at L32, Greater Hex 40% at L48, Curse 50% at L60.
    2. HoT the tank before the damage lands. Pact of Renewal is your main heal. Since 0.30.2.1 its ranks unlock 4 levels earlier (L8, 16, 24…) and deliver the same healing over a shorter duration.
    3. Buff: Berserker Spirit line (STR/STA/DEX/AGI), Pact of Haste, and Tribal Spirit (group version that uses Ritual Talismans at higher ranks).
    4. Debuff: Omen of Enfeeblement (STR/DEX/AGI) and Omen of Susceptibility (Disease/Holy/Magic resist; can't be resisted unless the target is immune to magic).
    5. Damage when safe: Ghostly Blight (magic DoT plus an initial hit), Malady/Sickness/Plague (disease DoT) and Spirit Bane/Blight (direct damage). Your DoTs stack with other Shamans' DoTs (deep dive).
    Stacking note (patch 0.30.0.0)

    Under the Necromancer section, the patch says: "The Malady spell line no longer allows multiple ranks to stack from the same caster." Shamans also have a Malady line. Whether this applies to the Shaman version is unconfirmed, so don't expect Malady and Greater Malady from you to stack.

    Strengths

    • Mana engine: Consume trades HP for mana (45 s cooldown after the rework). Consuming Pact (L12) restores mana for HP but greatly reduces incoming healing for 2 minutes (wiki).
    • Escape: Ritual of Escape (L40) clears threat for the party, hides you from living and undead, and heals and restores mana over 30 s.
    • Travel and utility: Gate, Soulbind, Speed of the Wild (L8) / Speed of the Pack (L32, group), Invisibility (L16, also grants See Invisible), Invisibility vs Undead (L8), Enduring Breath, Shrink, cures, Purge, Dispel Magic.
    • Group buffs on anyone: since 0.30.2.1, Tribal Spirit, Speed of the Pack and Communion of Regeneration can be cast on players outside your group.

    Limits

    • No pet, and burst damage is modest. Solo is "slower and more deliberate" (wiki Shaman Guides).
    • You're a steady healer, not a burst healer. Don't try to replace a Cleric's emergency heals.
    • Blind currently has a 60 s cooldown (temporary, 0.30.2.1, pending a Blind rework).

    Research and quest spells

    • The deep dive says Communion of Regeneration and the higher Tribal Spirit ranks come from spell research, and Aspect of the Bear comes from a Shaman quest during Early Access.
    • Patch 0.30.0.0 changed this: "Most group buffs are now available to purchase from spell vendors directly, rather than as research-granted spells." The wiki now lists Tribal Spirit/Rage/Fury and the Communion line as Vendor spells.
    • The wiki Shaman page (rev 80920, Oct 13) no longer lists the Aspect of the Bear spells at all (it used to list L28/40/52 as Research). How to get them in EA is unverified.
    • Talismans: Ritual Talisman, Greater Ritual Talisman and Ancestral Ritual Talisman are sold by a shaman scribe in the Seekers of the Lost Eye guild, Night Harbor (wiki merchant page). Prices are unconfirmed.
