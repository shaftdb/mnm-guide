Shaman

Shamans draw power from the spirit realm. The official deep dive frames them as enhancers and debilitators: they make the group hit harder, break enemies down with slows and Omens, and keep everyone topped up with heal-over-time spells. The combat pet is gone. Spirit Wolf and Summon Companion were removed in the rework, even though the wiki description still mentions "beastial spirits".

Your job in a group

  1. Slow first. The deep dive calls Omen of Lethargy "often the most useful thing you'll do in a hard fight." Wiki ranks: 10% at L4, Greater 20% at L16, Hex 30% at L32, Greater Hex 40% at L48, Curse 50% at L60.
  2. HoT the tank before the damage lands. Pact of Renewal is your main heal. Since 0.30.2.1 its ranks unlock 4 levels earlier (L8, 16, 24…) and deliver the same healing over a shorter duration.
  3. Buff: Berserker Spirit line (STR/STA/DEX/AGI), Pact of Haste, and Tribal Spirit (group version that uses Ritual Talismans at higher ranks).
  4. Debuff: Omen of Enfeeblement (STR/DEX/AGI) and Omen of Susceptibility (Disease/Holy/Magic resist; can't be resisted unless the target is immune to magic).
  5. Damage when safe: Ghostly Blight (magic DoT plus an initial hit), Malady/Sickness/Plague (disease DoT) and Spirit Bane/Blight (direct damage). Your DoTs stack with other Shamans' DoTs (deep dive).
Stacking note (patch 0.30.0.0)

Under the Necromancer section, the patch says: "The Malady spell line no longer allows multiple ranks to stack from the same caster." Shamans also have a Malady line. Whether this applies to the Shaman version is unconfirmed, so don't expect Malady and Greater Malady from you to stack.

Strengths

  • Mana engine: Consume trades HP for mana (45 s cooldown after the rework). Consuming Pact (L12) restores mana for HP but greatly reduces incoming healing for 2 minutes (wiki).
  • Escape: Ritual of Escape (L40) clears threat for the party, hides you from living and undead, and heals and restores mana over 30 s.
  • Travel and utility: Gate, Soulbind, Speed of the Wild (L8) / Speed of the Pack (L32, group), Invisibility (L16, also grants See Invisible), Invisibility vs Undead (L8), Enduring Breath, Shrink, cures, Purge, Dispel Magic.
  • Group buffs on anyone: since 0.30.2.1, Tribal Spirit, Speed of the Pack and Communion of Regeneration can be cast on players outside your group.

Limits

  • No pet, and burst damage is modest. Solo is "slower and more deliberate" (wiki Shaman Guides).
  • You're a steady healer, not a burst healer. Don't try to replace a Cleric's emergency heals.
  • Blind currently has a 60 s cooldown (temporary, 0.30.2.1, pending a Blind rework).

Research and quest spells

  • The deep dive says Communion of Regeneration and the higher Tribal Spirit ranks come from spell research, and Aspect of the Bear comes from a Shaman quest during Early Access.
  • Patch 0.30.0.0 changed this: "Most group buffs are now available to purchase from spell vendors directly, rather than as research-granted spells." The wiki now lists Tribal Spirit/Rage/Fury and the Communion line as Vendor spells.
  • The wiki lists Aspect of the Bear (L28), Savage Bear (L40) and Spirit Bear (L52) as Research. How to get them in EA is unconfirmed.
  • Talismans: Ritual Talisman, Greater Ritual Talisman and Ancestral Ritual Talisman are sold by a shaman scribe in the Seekers of the Lost Eye guild, Night Harbor (wiki merchant page). Prices are unconfirmed.
