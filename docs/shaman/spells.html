Pact of Renewal timing

The wiki now lists Pact of Renewal at L8 and Greater Pact of Renewal at L16, which matches patch 0.30.2.1 ("every rank now unlocks 4 levels earlier, starting at level 8"). Older guides, including the wiki's own Shaman Guides page, still say Pact of Renewal is level 12.
