Shaman quests

Shaman class quests (wiki)

  • Shaman Starter Quest (Night Harbor) — Night Harbor, giver Errol Shrewtender, min lvl 1. Rewards: Awakened Spirit Pouch, Dusty Spiritist Tunic
  • Shaman Starter Quest (Faelindral) — Faelindral, giver Varilis Thornvine, min lvl 1. Rewards: Novice Hoop of the Listener, Apprentice Hoop of the Listener, Spirit-Blessed Headdress, Robes of the Listener, Hoop of the Listener
  • Shaman Starter Quest (Underdocks) — Underdocks, giver Picci Manifold, min lvl 1. Rewards: Shredded Cloak, Petrified Undercap Stem, Mycelial Staff, Mycelial Spear
  • Necrolyte's Pouch Quest — Night Harbor, giver Bones, min lvl 1. Rewards: Necrolyte's Pouch
Aspect of the Bear quest

The official deep dive says Aspect of the Bear "will be rewarded from a Shaman quest during Early Access". The wiki Shaman page no longer lists it (it was a level-28 Research entry before rev 80920), and there is no wiki quest page for it. Treat both the source and the quest as unconfirmed.
