Tradeskills for a Shaman

Money first

Patch 0.30.2.4: "Tradeskills are expensive to get started on: characters that are focused on many tradeskills end up being money constrained." Spells are the Shaman's power, so put coin into scrolls first. strategy

Low-effort picks strategy

SkillWhy it suits a Shaman (or an alt army)Where to start (wiki)
SurvivalBandages (Bind Wound), torches and campfires. Crafted from your abilities with no bench neededTrainer: see the Tradeskills table
FishingPairs with Cooking, and you can do it while you wait for mana. Underdocks gained fishing volumes in 0.30.2.4Buy a pole from a fisherman
CookingMeals give temporary stat buffs and HP regen (wiki)Chef Argna (Shaded Bloom Inn) or Cooking Trainer Violyn (Night Harbor)
Bind WoundA free channeled HoT between pullsUse bandages from Survival
Herbalism → AlchemyGather herbs while leveling (sickle in the primary slot), and an alchemist alt can turn them into buffsHerbalism trainer in Sojourner's Camp. Alchemist Vizyl in Blackfeather Bazaar
RidingAvailable to any class from level 15Rider Ebraim, Sojourner's Camp

Skip for now: Brewing (the wiki marks it not implemented) and any skill with an "Example" recipe table on the wiki.
