0.30.2.5 - Module 1 Patch 8

October 07, 2026

  • Purchasing houses in Night Harbor East where the Realtor is in Night Harbor West should now work
  • We've updated the cost of spell scrolls for a smoother ramp from levels 1-60, and an overall lower total cost to buy all your spells and abilties.
  • You should find your scrolls are available more inexpensively for the level range you acquire them.
  • Spells are intended to be a goal that you save up for, so while they are less expensive than before they will still be an investment.
  • We're looking into giving some coin back to players who bought scrolls that are now cheaper after the update. We'll gather the data and try to get this done in the near future.
  • PvP - reduced melee and ranged-caused snares to a duration of 12 seconds; these also have PvP diminishing returns when reused on the same target
  • We've removed the ability to disenchant some low level quest items that were very easy to get for the materials they provided.
  • Some poisonous creatures now have stronger poison
  • As mentioned in a prior patch note, Cureberries are now forageable for Druids and Rangers and no longer summoned. We have removed the Summoned Curerberries scroll from spell vendors.
  • We found a few quests that needed some balancing so the rewards better matched the effort required, and have made adjustments to a few of them.
  • Father Rau requested a quest item that was not dropping correctly,and this has also been fixed
  • Reenabled NPCs after fixing their rewards
  • Ratan in The Bends
  • a friendly undercap in Underdocks
  • Delilah in Underdocks
  • Fixed misattributed Underdocks faction on one NPC in the zone
  • Fixed Hal'kareth (SPB & WIZ) house quest bug
  • Fixed missing spawn command for newbie yard spawner
  • Added HC, Solo and Self Found NPCs
