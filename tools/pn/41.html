0.28.1.0 - Beta Patch 9 Hotfix 1

August 22, 2026

  • Possible fix to spellbook desync issue
  • Possible fix for spellbooks failing to scribe all their spells via Gedric
  • Recipes on merchants now show their "white difficulty" level
  • Pustulax now properly resets when the server reboots
  • Fixed various AOE ability issues
  • Fixes Bane of the Twin Drakes proc
  • Fixes Scintallation
  • I am sure many more were affected
  • All instant abilities can now be used while moving
  • Disabled VSync setting completely until we can fix its performance
  • Bug fix for PvP taunt giving immunity before cycling through all ability effects
  • fix dying to PvP dots post zoning causing xp death\
  • Freezing cold no longer hits you everywhere like a blizzard
  • Zoneline added to the Keep and Cave, leading to Shaded Dunes
  • Teleporter NPC within Rothold removed
  • Dryad NPCs have been re-adjusted
  • Multiple NPCs had their social behavior adjusted
  • Adjusted some spawns
  • Fixed the barrel exploit
  • Ongoing tuning works
  • New zone lines added down the ravine
  • We have set a maximum on non priority particles that can appear on your screen
  • Added Anchored Tooltips: hovering over UI elements, NPCs, crafting stations, and ground items now displays contextual information
  • Tooltips can be toggled with F9 (rebindable) or in Settings > Interface
  • Tooltip position is saved between sessions
  • Fixed starting scroll for new druids to give Spark of Nature, rather than the deprecated Minor Heal
  • Holy Strike -Decreased the amount of bonus damage on the Righteous Strike proc at low-levels of Wisdom. These procs still have the same possible max hit amounts, but require a bit more Wisdom to scale up the bonus damage.
