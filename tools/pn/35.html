0.26.0.2 - Beta Patch 7 Hotfix 2

July 14, 2026

  • Party members can now always see stealthed group mates
  • Rogue abilities have new icons
  • Removed a hidden extra 6 seconds on Pet Provoke cooldowns
  • Deextinction (BST/DRU) can now resurrect mounts up to level 39
  • Allow sitting while swimming (and medding in Sunken Resort)
  • Fixed abilities that cause player movement such as Charge and Pounce
  • Fixes to threat generation and reduction
  • Fix BST exploit where you can dismiss then recall your pet to clear cooldowns
  • Fix campfires and caltrops lasting until zone/death if they expire while in them
  • Fix item being stuck on cursor due to unique collision between bank and inventory
  • Skirmisher speed bonus increased from 5% -> 10%
  • Howl of Dominance moved to its own cooldown, off of the Martial cooldown
  • Pounce movement has been fixed
  • Icons updated on several abilities
  • Invigorate Companion is not longer on a Global Cooldown
  • Using Call Companion now puts all the pet's abilities on cooldown
  • Prowler's Instinct no longer breaks on damage or action
  • Maul is now obtained at level 10 as it more or less requires Dual Wield to be effective.  Command: Kill has moved to level 9.
  • Kinship: Bat has moved to level 30 for better pacing of ability acquisition.
  • Guardian Spirit can no longer be cast while moving
  • Correct version of Fear Beasts and Shrink Companion added to Beastmaster spell merchant
  • Kick scroll added to Beastmaster spell merchant
  • Fixed an issue with Guardian Spirit where it was not correctly applying the damage reduction
  • Rogue reagents vendor now sells vials for use in Poison Making
  • Fixed level-based scaling of the Evade ability's Threat reduction
  • Icons updated on several abilities
  • The Stealth bonus to Backstab now doubles the flat damage portion as well as the weapon damage portion.
  • The Flat damage portion of Backstab has been increased from tiers 2-6, from 20/30/40/50/60 to 25/45/70/100/200
  • Slice Damage per tick has been reduced 20% at tiers 2-6, down to 8/24/48/58/72
  • Proc'd poisons can now stack 1 from each family of rogue poison
  • Twin Blades will now commute stacks of Slice on the target, dealing additional damage based on the number of stacks.
  • Party members will now be able to see and target you while you are in Stealth
  • Toxin extraction for poison making will now extract twice as much toxin from the components.
  • Rogue instructor now sells Evade scroll.
  • Using Conduit in Arcane Infusion will no longer cause a Global Cooldown
  • Fixed poisonous wasp sting assignment levels
  • Adjusted some NPC ability damage values
  • Removed Charge ability from undead NPCs
  • Adjusted NPC Charge ability conditions and cooldowns
  • Adjusted NPC Kick cooldowns
  • Glinting Hollow entrance key will now persist on the player through death
  • Fix for a side step of the ashira faction quest
