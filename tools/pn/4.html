0.20.25.1 - Hotfixes

February 27, 2026

  • Change meditation to award partial meditation mana per tick based on how long you have been sitting in that tick
  • Fix to framerate hit due to chat spam
  • Raise minimum swimming speed to 40% of run speed
  • Prevent searching for items to stack from considering account bank slot
  • Profanity filter's ass has been looked at and rectified
  • Profanity filter will no longer apply to non-chat messages
  • Prevent stabling and hitching dead mounts
  • Inventory fixes
  • Can no longer remove saddle while mounted
  • Bank coin fixes
  • Various fixes to edge cases relating to mounts that can prevent dismounting or zoning
  • You no longer get a breath bar whenever your stamina increases
  • You can no longer unhitch other players' mounts
  • When looking for items in a mount's inventory, it will consider items in saddlebags as well
  • Minimum level for riding skill is now visible on the riding trainer's interface
  • Can no longer sow your mount and benefit from that sow while riding it
  • Fix some NPC ability target selection logic
  • Fix restoration of persistent pet xp
  • Correctly dismounting in no mount volumes
  • Fix campfire effects not being removed even when campfire expires
  • Fix traded items that were supposed to end up in containers instead ending up in your face or other strange places
  • Revert a lot of inventory performance facing changes that were the cause of the inventory issues. We will polish them again and rerelease them at a later time
  • Prevent errors in pet window while reloading
  • Workaround for not being able to place items in mount bags after /reload. May still cause items to visually appear incorrect
  • Pet rename issues fixed on zone or reload
  • Profanity list adjustments
  • Night harbor fixes to occlusion culling issues
  • Keeper's Bight waterfalls now launch you
  • UI Reset version detection, and updated version forcing a reset
  • Night harbor Line of Sight fixes
  • Bonded BST pets will now show their level in the pet window
  • Fixed an issue where attempting to call your dismissed bonded pet with another primary pet active would bug your bonded pet until you zoned
  • BST pets will now show their abilities in the pet window immediately upon taming
  • Latest itemization and content changes have been merged
  • New item types have been introduced
  • Drop rates have been adjusted across almost all rare mobs
  • New crafting recipes relating to alchemy and other tradeskills
  • Crafted Jewelry AC has been reduced
